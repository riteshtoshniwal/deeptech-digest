🔐 Cybersecurity

CallPhantom: 28 Fake Android Apps Hit 7.3 Million Downloads, Primarily Targeting India

ESET Research uncovered a large-scale Android scam — dubbed CallPhantom — involving 28 fraudulent apps on Google Play that falsely claimed to provide call histories, SMS records, and WhatsApp call logs for any phone number. The apps collectively accumulated 7.3 million downloads (one app alone: 3M+), and primarily targeted users in India and the Asia-Pacific region. India's +91 country code was pre-selected in the apps and UPI payment support was included, underscoring India as the primary target. 53.7% of all CallPhantom detections worldwide were recorded in India. ESET — as an App Defense Alliance partner — reported its findings to Google, which removed all identified apps. (Published: 2026-05-08)

China-Linked Hackers Target Asian Governments, NATO State, Journalists & Activists

Trend Micro disclosed an ongoing China-aligned espionage campaign (tracked as SHADOW-EARTH-053) targeting government and defense sectors across South, East, and Southeast Asia — including India, Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, and Pakistan — as well as Poland (NATO). Attackers exploited N-day vulnerabilities in Microsoft Exchange Server and IIS systems, deployed Godzilla web shells, and installed ShadowPad malware via DLL side-loading. A parallel track targeted Uyghur, Tibetan, Taiwanese, and Hong Kong dissidents and investigative journalists via spear-phishing. (Published: 2026-05-01)

A new analysis in The Diplomat highlights a critical gap: while AI capabilities are advancing rapidly across Southeast Asia, the region's cybersecurity policy frameworks are lagging significantly. Despite Vietnam's new AI Law (effective March 2026) and ASEAN's governance principles, binding enforcement mechanisms remain largely absent, leaving critical infrastructure exposed. India Deeptech Funding Surpasses $936M in 2026

Tracxn data shows India's deeptech companies raised $936 million in equity funding across 74 rounds in 2026 (through April). The government's February 2026 policy revision — extending deeptech startup status to 20 years and raising the revenue threshold for tax/grant benefits to ₹3 billion — continues to attract investment. Southeast Asia Deeptech Startups Turn to Japan for Capital & Growth

A report from KR Asia reveals that SE Asian science- and research-driven startups are increasingly turning to Japan as their primary expansion destination, driven by weak regional funding and small domestic markets. Nearly 70 startups and organizations from Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, and beyond participated in Tokyo's SusHi Tech conference. Raisewell Ventures founder Jeep Kline noted that "deeptech in SE Asia takes longer" and fund structures should reflect 12–15 year horizons. (Published: 2026 Q2)

🏛️ Tech Policy & Regulation

India's IT Amendment Rules 2026: 3-Hour Takedown, Mandatory AI Content Labeling

India's new draft IT Rules amendments have introduced two major changes: a three-hour takedown deadline for harmful content on social media platforms (down from 24–36 hours), and mandatory disclosure and labeling of synthetic/AI-generated content. Critics warn this could remove safe harbour protections for Meta, Google, and X, exposing platforms to direct legal liability. Concerns also exist about the expansion of oversight to individual users sharing news content. (Published: 2026, ongoing)

Vietnam's AI Law Enters Implementation Phase

Vietnam — the first country in Southeast Asia to enact a binding AI law — entered its first implementation phase in March 2026, establishing regulatory infrastructure and appointing oversight authorities. The law is being rolled out in phases over four years. Other SE Asian nations, including Philippines (which aims to present an AI regulatory framework to ASEAN) and Malaysia, are watching closely as they develop their own frameworks. (Published: ongoing, 2026)

🔬 Key Takeaways & Insights

1. India as AI Manufacturing Hub: Google's exploration of server and drone manufacturing in India signals a shift from India being purely a consumer market and services provider to becoming an AI hardware manufacturing destination — a major strategic pivot enabled by the government's "Make in AI" push.

2. Mobile Fraud Targeting India at Scale: The CallPhantom campaign underscores a persistent and growing threat: Indian mobile users are disproportionately targeted by subscription fraud on app stores. With 53.7% of global detections in India and UPI-integrated payment flows, this is a sophisticated, market-specific attack vector that demands more rigorous app store review processes.

3. State-Sponsored Cyber Espionage Intensifies in Asia: The SHADOW-EARTH-053 campaign reflects the normalisation of China-linked APT activity targeting South and Southeast Asian governments, with India squarely in the crosshairs. Defence and government sector CISOs must prioritise patching Exchange Server/IIS vulnerabilities as these remain the primary initial access vector.

4. SE Asia Deeptech Funding Gap Pushes Startups Outward: The structural underfunding of deeptech in SE Asia is driving its best companies to seek capital and markets in Japan and beyond. Regional VCs need to adapt with longer fund durations to compete for these assets.

5. Regulatory Tightening Across the Region: From India's IT Rules mandating AI content labeling to Vietnam's phased AI Law implementation, governments are closing the gap between AI capability and regulatory oversight — though enforcement capacity remains a critical bottleneck.
