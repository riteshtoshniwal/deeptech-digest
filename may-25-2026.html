🛡️ CYBERSECURITY

Laravel-Lang PHP Packages Hit by Major Supply Chain Attack

A sophisticated supply chain attack compromised the popular Laravel-Lang Composer package ecosystem, affecting over 700 versions across four packages: laravel-lang/lang, laravel-lang/attributes, laravel-lang/http-statuses, and laravel-lang/actions. Discovered by Aikido Security on May 22, the attacker—who had push access to the Laravel-Lang GitHub organization—rewrote every git tag within a 15-minute window to point to malicious commits in an attacker-controlled fork. The injected payload automatically exfiltrates CI/CD secrets, cloud keys, Kubernetes tokens, SSH material, environment files, browser data, password manager vaults, and crypto wallets to the domain flipboxstudio[.]info. PHP developers worldwide, including across India's large Laravel user base, are urged to audit their dependencies immediately. (Published: 2026-05-22)

BELFOR Asia Ransomware Attack: Update 2 Issued

Following the April 27, 2026 ransomware attack by the INC ransomware group against BELFOR (Asia) Pte Ltd in Singapore, the company has issued a second update confirming it has successfully migrated to a new ISO 27001-certified IT environment. India's Quantum Computing Chip Breakthrough Announced

India has announced a significant milestone in quantum computing chip development, aligning with the National Quantum Mission (NQM) — a ₹6,003.65 crore (~$730M) initiative targeting 50–1,000 qubit systems by 2031. The new chip demonstrates enhanced qubit stability and improved error-correction protocols, enabling researchers to tackle computational tasks previously unmanageable with classical silicon processors. The development is part of India's broader strategy to achieve technological self-reliance in quantum, reducing dependence on costly international hardware while keeping sensitive research data sovereign. 🏛️ POLICY & REGULATION

India IT Rules 2026: AI Content Labelling and Deepfake Crackdown Now in Force

India's amended IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, notified in February 2026, are now being enforced with increasing scrutiny. The rules mandate prominent labelling or metadata embedding for AI-generated "Synthetically Generated Information" (SGI), a 3-hour takedown deadline for harmful synthetic content, and traceability requirements for Significant Social Media Intermediaries. The rules form part of a broader 2026 digital governance push that also includes the DPDP Rules, the RBI's AI-in-financial-services framework, and the CCI's market study on AI — collectively marking India's formal alignment with the AI era. ADB Launches $70 Billion Push for Asia's Digital and Energy Infrastructure

The Asian Development Bank announced it will back $70 billion in new energy and digital infrastructure across Asia and the Pacific by 2035. The plan comprises two major programmes: a $50 billion Pan-Asia Power Grid Initiative focused on cross-border electricity infrastructure, and a $20 billion Asia-Pacific Digital Highway to finance AI-ready digital corridors, subsea fibre networks, satellite links, and regional data centres. The initiative aims to bring first-time broadband access to 200 million people, improve connectivity for 450 million more, cut costs in remote areas by 40%, and create 4 million jobs — with Southeast Asia identified as the primary beneficiary. 💡 KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • Supply chain attacks are the new frontier: The Laravel-Lang compromise is a stark reminder that open-source package ecosystems remain highly vulnerable — developers across India and Southeast Asia's large PHP/Laravel communities must audit dependencies urgently.

  • India's quantum and sovereign AI push is materialising: With the NQM delivering a chip breakthrough and Sarvam AI advancing its 120B-parameter sovereign LLM, India is making credible progress toward high-tech self-reliance.

  • Southeast Asia's cyber defences lag AI threat velocity: As AI-generated malware outpaces detection tools and the APAC cybersecurity talent gap widens, regional governments and enterprises face mounting pressure to invest in defensive capabilities.

  • Capital is concentrating, not contracting: Across both India and Southeast Asia, funding is bifurcating — late-stage and deep tech companies are attracting larger cheques, while seed-stage capital remains scarce. Founders need strong unit economics to compete.

  • Infrastructure is the new battleground: The ADB's $70B commitment and Malaysia's data centre dominance signal that Asia's AI future will be won or lost at the infrastructure layer — connectivity, compute, and energy.
