🤝 India-US Eye Quantum, AI & Nuclear as Next Cooperation Frontier

India's Ambassador to the US, Vinay Mohan Kwatra, confirmed at the US-India AI and Emerging Technology Forum on May 9, 2026 that quantum technologies, artificial intelligence, and nuclear science are among the next frontier areas of bilateral cooperation. Kwatra highlighted nuclear fusion combined with AI optimisation and quantum computing as a particularly promising area, and noted that a delegation of American nuclear companies will visit India later this month. India is pushing for the "democratisation of AI" while expanding its trusted technology partnership with the United States, with emphasis on safe and secure AI for infrastructure protection. 🌏 Microsoft Report: Asia Leads Global AI Adoption Surge

Microsoft's Q1 2026 Global AI Diffusion Report, published May 7, 2026, shows that twelve of the fifteen fastest-growing economies for AI adoption are in Asia, driven largely by improved AI model performance in local languages. South Korea (+43%), Thailand (+36%), and Japan (+34%) are leading the expansion. Global AI usage has reached 17.8% of the world's working-age population, while Southeast Asia is growing faster than the global average, with a significant share of organisations moving beyond pilot projects to scaled AI deployment. The report also warns that the AI adoption gap between the Global North and Global South is widening. (Published: 2026-05-07)

🔐 CYBERSECURITY: CallPhantom Scam Hits 7.3 Million Android Users — India the Primary Target

ESET Research uncovered a large-scale Android fraud campaign dubbed CallPhantom that affected 28 malicious apps on Google Play, collectively downloaded over 7.3 million times before removal. The apps falsely claimed to provide access to call histories, SMS records, and WhatsApp call logs for any phone number — charging users a subscription fee in return for randomly generated fake data. India was the hardest-hit country, accounting for 53.7% of global detections; the apps came pre-configured with India's +91 country code and supported UPI payments. ESET reported the findings to Google as an App Defense Alliance partner, and all 28 apps have been removed from the Play Store. (Published: 2026-05-07)

🔐 CYBERSECURITY: cPanel Flaw CVE-2026-41940 Weaponised Against Southeast Asian Governments

A critical authentication bypass vulnerability in cPanel and WebHost Manager (WHM), tracked as CVE-2026-41940, has been actively weaponised by an unknown threat actor targeting government and military entities in Southeast Asia — specifically the Philippines (including the coast guard) and Laos — as well as managed service providers in Canada, South Africa, and the US. The flaw was first detected by Ctrl-Alt-Intel on May 2, 2026; within 24 hours, ransomware was deployed against vulnerable servers. At peak exploitation, at least 44,000 IP addresses were observed scanning and brute-forcing servers. Attacks have led to website defacements, data encryption, and malware deployment across more than 40,000 servers globally. Administrators are urged to patch immediately. (Published: 2026-05-04)

🔐 CYBERSECURITY: China-Linked SHADOW-EARTH-053 Targets Asian Governments, Defence Sectors

Cybersecurity firm Trend Micro disclosed a sophisticated China-aligned espionage campaign — tracked as SHADOW-EARTH-053 — targeting government and defence sectors across South, East, and Southeast Asia (including India, Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, and Pakistan), and one NATO European government (Poland). Active since at least December 2024, the group exploits N-day vulnerabilities in internet-facing Microsoft Exchange and IIS servers, deploys the Godzilla web shell for persistent access, and installs ShadowPad malware via DLL sideloading. Notably, nearly half the SHADOW-EARTH-053 targets in Malaysia, Sri Lanka, and Myanmar were also previously compromised by the related cluster SHADOW-EARTH-054. Separately, two Chinese-linked groups — GLITTER CARP and SEQUIN CARP — ran phishing campaigns against journalists and civil society activists. (Published: 2026-05-01)

⚠️ Southeast Asia Faces Cyber Spillover Risk From Iran Conflict

Security experts are warning that Southeast Asia faces growing cyber spillover risk from the widening US-Israel-Iran conflict, with state-linked hackers and criminal groups seeking to exploit disruptions in energy, shipping, and banking networks well beyond the Middle East. Iran has stated it will target economic and banking interests linked to the United States and Israel across the region; researchers noted a surge of approximately 60 hacktivist groups active in the conflict, including pro-Russian factions. Malaysia — Iran's closest regional ally with extensive political and economic ties and the largest Persian diaspora in Southeast Asia — is considered particularly exposed. Critical infrastructure across South and Southeast Asia faces elevated risk as economic spillover effects compound the cyber threat landscape. (Published: 2026-05-09)

🤖 Anthropic Launches 10 AI Agents for Financial Services — Implications for Asia's Fintech Sector

On May 5, 2026, Anthropic announced a suite of 10 pre-built AI agents targeting banks, insurers, and asset managers, alongside Claude Opus 4.7 — its most capable model for financial work. The agents cover pitch deck generation, earnings review, financial model building, KYC screening, general ledger reconciliation, and compliance case escalation. The announcement is significant for India and Southeast Asia's rapidly growing fintech sectors; India's UPI-connected fintech ecosystem and Singapore's status as a regional financial hub make both markets prime candidates for early adoption. FactSet shares fell 8.1% and Morningstar erased gains following the news, signalling disruption to financial data incumbents. (Published: 2026-05-05)

🚀 India Deeptech Ecosystem: $936M Raised in 2026 YTD, 71% Jump vs 2025

India's deeptech startup funding momentum continues strongly into 2026, with $936 million raised across 74 equity funding rounds through April, representing a 71.29% increase compared to the same period in 2025. The government's DPIIT policy reforms from February 2026, which extended recognition for deeptech startups to 20 years and raised the turnover threshold to ₹300 crore, are cited as a key enabler. The IndiaAI Compute Portal is offering GPU compute at ₹65/hour versus the global rate of ₹210–250/hour, with 20,000 additional GPUs being added to the existing 38,000-unit pool. The India Deep Tech Alliance's inaugural report also fast-tracked $1 billion USD in AI startup investment commitments. (Published: 2026-05-09)

📋 Key Takeaways & Insights

Geopolitical tech alignment is accelerating. The India-US AI forum signals that quantum, AI, and nuclear collaboration is moving from aspiration to concrete partnerships, with private sector delegations and formal frameworks on the way.

Asia is the fastest-growing AI adoption region globally, but the widening gap between advanced and emerging economies within the region is a structural risk that policymakers and investors must address.

Cybersecurity threats in the region are intensifying on multiple fronts: state-sponsored espionage (China-linked SHADOW-EARTH), criminal fraud at massive scale (CallPhantom), weaponised critical infrastructure vulnerabilities (cPanel CVE-2026-41940), and geopolitical spillover (Iran conflict) are converging simultaneously.

India remains the primary consumer-facing fraud target in Asia — the CallPhantom campaign's 53.7% India concentration underscores the need for stronger mobile app vetting mechanisms and public digital literacy campaigns.

Southeast Asian governments urgently need to patch critical vulnerabilities. The cPanel flaw was exploited within 24 hours of disclosure; government IT teams in the Philippines, Laos, and beyond must prioritise patch management and attack surface reduction.

AI is reshaping financial services globally, and India and Southeast Asia's fintech sectors should expect rapid disruption as Anthropic, OpenAI, and others deploy specialised financial AI agents that can automate KYC, compliance, and deal workflows.
