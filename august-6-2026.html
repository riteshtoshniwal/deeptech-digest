AI & Machine Learning

Anthropic Confirms In-House Team to Build Custom AI Chips for Claude

Anthropic officially confirmed it is building an in-house silicon engineering team to develop custom AI chips for its Claude models. The move comes as demand for Claude surges, with the company betting that specialized silicon can improve model performance, reduce inference costs, and optimize energy efficiency — a development watched closely by India and SEA enterprises building on frontier models. ByteDance Founder Bans Employees From Distilling U.S. Frontier AI Models

ByteDance's founder has barred employees from distilling U.S. frontier AI models, signalling a sharper decoupling in Asia's AI ecosystem as Chinese labs push to build independent capability. The move has implications for AI supply chains and model-access strategies across Asian markets, including Indian and Southeast Asian developers who build atop both U.S. and Chinese model families. (Published: 2026-08-05)
- Lapaas Voice
- Wccftech

Tech Mahindra Uses AI to Cut Healthcare Technical Debt by 30–35%

Tech Mahindra reported that its AI-led services are helping healthcare organizations reduce technical debt by 30–35%, automating large portions of application modernization, code analysis, testing and documentation. The approach lets hospitals, insurers and life-sciences firms shift resources from maintaining legacy IT to building new digital capabilities — a notable proof point for India's AI-in-IT-services story. (Published: 2026-08-05)
- Lapaas Voice

Cybersecurity

CISA Flags Three Actively Exploited Vulnerabilities Relevant to APAC Enterprises

The U.S. CISA added three flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on August 5, citing active exploitation in the wild: CVE-2026-9198 (CVSS 9.8, unauthenticated remote code execution in Langflow — a widely used open-source AI agent-building tool), CVE-2026-34486 (Apache Tomcat cluster encryption bypass), and CVE-2026-18556 (N-able N-central authentication bypass). Langflow and Tomcat are heavily deployed across Indian and Southeast Asian enterprise and startup stacks, making prompt patching advisable for regional security teams. (Published: 2026-08-05)
- The Hacker News

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

Vaaree Raises ₹65 Cr ($6.8M) Series A Led by Hero Enterprise and Cap Alpha Ventures

Bengaluru-based home décor and furnishings marketplace Vaaree closed a ₹65 crore (~$6.8M) Series A co-led by Hero Enterprise and Cap Alpha Ventures. The capital will expand its fulfilment network and strengthen its AI-led styling and discovery technology, including its proprietary "VibeCheck" home-styling experience. Existing backers include Peak XV's Surge, PeerCapital, All In Capital, Better Capital and Kunal Shah. DealStreetAsia's India deals roundup published August 5 led with this transaction. Regional Tech Policy & Regulation

RBI to Pilot ₹10 and ₹20 Polymer Notes Ahead of Planned FY28 Rollout

The Reserve Bank of India announced a pilot of ₹10 and ₹20 polymer banknotes ahead of a planned FY28 rollout — a materials-technology shift aimed at durability and counterfeit resistance in India's currency infrastructure. (Published: 2026-08-05)
- Lapaas Voice

TCS Layoffs Reach 23,460 for FY26; Government Meeting Set for August 6

Analysis published August 5 confirmed via company filings that TCS closed March 2026 with 584,519 employees, a net fall of 23,460 — nearly double the ~12,200 cuts it had publicly flagged — making it the single largest source of global tech job cuts in 2026. The Karnataka government has sought an explanation over the layoffs, MeitY is monitoring the situation, and a meeting between TCS and government representatives was slated for August 6. (Published: 2026-08-05)
- Lapaas Voice
- Deccan Herald

Key Takeaways

  1. AI hardware independence is the theme of the day — Anthropic's custom-chip confirmation and ByteDance's distillation ban both point to frontier AI players reducing dependence on external ecosystems, with knock-on effects for Indian and SEA companies that build on these models.
  2. India's IT-services AI transition is producing both wins and pain — Tech Mahindra's 30–35% technical-debt reduction showcases AI's upside for Indian IT, while TCS's 23,460 net workforce reduction (with government scrutiny intensifying ahead of the August 6 meeting) shows the structural cost of the same transition.
  3. AI-tooling supply chains are now attack surfaces — the actively exploited Langflow RCE (CVSS 9.8) is a reminder that the open-source AI agent stack adopted rapidly across India/SEA startups needs the same patch discipline as traditional enterprise software.
  4. Consumer-tech funding in India continues at steady clip — Vaaree's ₹65 Cr Series A, with AI-led styling as the stated use of funds, reflects investors' preference for AI-differentiated consumer plays even in traditional categories like home décor.
