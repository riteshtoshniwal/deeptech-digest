🔐 Cybersecurity

India Hands Insurers an Emergency 3-Day AI Cyber Deadline

India's insurance regulator, IRDAI, issued an urgent directive requiring all insurers to submit a formal Action Taken Report (ATR) on frontier-AI cybersecurity readiness by Friday May 22 — just three working days after the order was issued. The directive instructs insurance companies to evaluate their posture against risks from advanced AI systems, detail preventive, detective, and responsive security measures, and report compliance to boards. The trigger: growing industry concern over AI-powered exploit-generation capabilities and autonomous cyber tools. IRDAI also cited CERT-In warnings about critical SAP vulnerabilities widely used in the insurance sector. Analysts warn the compressed timeline could split the market between tech-savvy carriers and smaller players struggling with legacy IT estates. (Published: 2026-05-19)

🤖 AI & Machine Learning

Singapore's April Exports Surge 24.5% on AI Semiconductor Demand

Singapore's non-oil domestic exports (NODX) jumped 24.5% year-on-year in April 2026, dramatically outpacing economist forecasts of 10.9% growth, driven by surging AI-related demand for semiconductors, disk media products, and personal computers. Integrated circuits soared 82.7%, disk media products 148.9%, and personal computers 35.7%. Exports to the United States climbed 59.6%, China 37.8%, and the European Union 33.4% — all reversing prior declines. Enterprise Singapore attributed the strong performance primarily to electronic exports amid sustained demand linked to artificial intelligence technologies. The results extend a trend of AI-cycle-driven hardware demand reshaping Asia's export landscape. (Published: 2026-05-19)

💰 Investments & M&A

Prudential Acquires 75% Stake in Bharti Life Insurance for $365M

Prudential plc announced it will acquire a 75% controlling stake in Bharti Life Insurance Company Limited from Bharti Life Ventures Pvt Ltd and 360 ONE Asset Management, paying ₹3,500 crore (approximately $365M) upfront with an additional earn-out of up to ₹700 crore. The deal marks a major strategic shift for Prudential — moving from minority to majority positions in India's large, underpenetrated life insurance market. Post-closing, Prudential will hold majority-owned entities in Bharti Life and Prudential HCL Health Insurance, plus minority positions in ICICI Prudential Asset Management and ICICI Prudential Life Insurance. The transaction aligns with India's "Insurance for All by 2047" government target. Regulatory hurdles include reducing Prudential's ICICIPru Life stake below 10%. Q1 2026 new business profit climbed 10% YoY to $686M. (Published: 2026-05-19 — deal announced May 17)

🌏 Southeast Asia Tech

Malaysia's AI-Powered Scam Epidemic: RM 2.77 Billion Lost in 2025

Malaysia's Deputy Minister of Communications Teo Nie Ching revealed that online scam losses reached RM 2.77 billion (approximately $700M USD) in 2025, up sharply from RM 1.28 billion in 2023 and RM 1.57 billion in 2024. Investment scams accounted for the largest share. Scammers are increasingly deploying deepfake voices, AI-generated images, and fake websites that mimic real brands to steal credentials and one-time codes. In response, Malaysia's Ministry of Digital launched MY-AI Standards in March 2026 — a platform providing businesses access to 80+ global AI standards — and is developing an AI-based image/video authenticity verification tool in collaboration with Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia. (Published: 2026-05-17, widely reported May 19)

📊 India Deeptech & Startup Ecosystem

India #1 Ransomware Target in APAC: 165% YoY Surge in Q1 2026

A report by cybersecurity firm Cyble identified India as the most targeted country in Asia-Pacific for ransomware attacks in Q1 2026, recording 45 incidents — a 165% year-on-year and 55% sequential increase. The Gentleman ransomware group accounted for nearly 24% of all incidents, with Qilin and INC Ransom among the most active operators. IT, manufacturing, healthcare, BFSI, automotive, and professional services were the hardest-hit sectors. CRIL recorded 277 major cyber incidents across APAC in Q1, including ransomware, data breaches, compromised access sales, and hacktivist activity. Government and law enforcement organisations recorded the highest number of data breach incidents. Underground forums advertised leaked Indian enterprise data, including one incident involving alleged administrator-level access to a major Indian construction company's 44GB database. (Published: 2026-05-14)

🏛️ Tech Policy & Regulation

India IT Sector: AI, Cloud & Cybersecurity Now Drive 65% of Tech Hiring

A new Foundit report shows India's IT sector hiring has pivoted sharply, with AI/ML, cloud, and cybersecurity now driving approximately 65% of all tech hiring demand — and AI-first roles accounting for over 30% of new tech positions. The IT sector is expected to expand 6% year-on-year in 2026, with Global Capability Centres (GCCs) projected to create around 132,000 new jobs. Skill-based hiring is forecast to surpass 70% adoption, with Tier II cities accounting for ~40% of additional hiring. Delhi-NCR has emerged as the fastest-growing major IT hub with 28% YoY growth. Premium compensation is being commanded by skills with low automation risk including AI/ML, cybersecurity, cloud, and generative AI. (Published: May 2026)
