IMD Launches India's First AI-Powered Block-Level Monsoon Forecasting System

The India Meteorological Department (IMD), in collaboration with the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM, Pune) and the National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF), launched two AI-enabled monsoon advance forecasting systems on May 12, 2026. The systems can predict monsoon onset at the block level up to four weeks in advance, with a spatial resolution of 1 km grid — covering over 3,000 sub-districts across 16 states. Vietnam's AI Law Takes Effect — Southeast Asia's First National AI Legislation

Vietnam's comprehensive AI Law, promulgated in December 2025, began its phased rollout from March 2026, making Vietnam the first country in Southeast Asia to enact dedicated national AI legislation. As of May 2026, industry stakeholders are assessing compliance gaps, with the law being closely watched by neighbouring Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia as a regional benchmark.
