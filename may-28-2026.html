🛡️ Cybersecurity

CERT-In Sets 12-Hour Patching Deadline to Counter AI-Accelerated Cyber Attacks

India's Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) released a landmark cybersecurity framework on May 27, mandating that organisations patch or mitigate known-exploited vulnerabilities on internet-facing or crown-jewel systems within 12 hours where feasible. The directive reflects how AI-assisted exploitation has dramatically reduced the time attackers need to identify and weaponise vulnerabilities—insecure APIs, exposed cloud services, and weak identities are now at risk faster than ever. CERT-In's framework sets a tiered response timeline: critical internal vulnerabilities must be addressed within 24 hours, high-severity issues within 5 days. (Published: 2026-05-27)

Indian Government & Tech Firms Join Forces to Test Anthropic's Claude Mythos

India has taken a proactive stance on frontier AI cybersecurity risk: the Indian government and partnering tech firms have begun stress-testing Anthropic's powerful new cybersecurity model, Claude Mythos, to assess vulnerabilities in critical systems including the Aadhaar national ID infrastructure and government authentication platforms. The move follows Mythos having identified over 23,000 software vulnerabilities and 10,000+ critical flaws during early access testing by global organisations. India is reportedly negotiating a secure bilateral testing arrangement with the US. (Published: 2026-05-27)

🚀 Deeptech Startups & Investment

India's Deeptech Ecosystem Hits $931M in Funding in Early 2026—Up 70%

India's deeptech sector has raised $931 million across 79 rounds in 2026 through April—a 69.85% jump versus the same period in 2025. AI accounts for the dominant share, with robotics, semiconductors, and defence tech also attracting capital. The India Deep Tech Alliance's inaugural report confirmed AI funding specifically jumped 58% in 2025, and the government is offering 100% compute subsidies to startups building foundational AI models under the IndiaAI Mission. India now hosts 4,200+ deeptech startups, with 550 new companies added in the past year. (Published: 2026-05-26)

Red Balloon Aerospace Tests India's First Near-Space Commercial Balloon Platform

Vijayawada-based Red Balloon Aerospace successfully tested its VISTA platform—India's first near-space commercial super-pressure balloon—rising nearly 25 kilometres above Earth carrying payloads from seven Indian and international partners. The mission validates a low-cost stratospheric delivery platform for rural telecommunications, disaster monitoring, atmospheric research, and agriculture surveillance. India joins a small group of nations with indigenous stratospheric balloon capability. (Published: 2026-05-27)

India's Space Tech Sector Pivots from Launches to Revenue

A wide-ranging analysis published May 27 examines India's 400+ space startups shifting from technology demonstration to commercial viability. Companies including Pixxel (hyperspectral imaging for NASA's CSDA programme), Skyroot Aerospace (preparing orbital launches), and Agnikul are racing to build sustainable revenue from defence contracts, satellite intelligence, and enterprise earth-observation products. The piece notes the sector faces a critical "proof of business" phase after years of proof-of-concept work. (Published: 2026-05-27)

📋 Tech Policy & Regulation

India's 2026 IT Rules: AI Content Labelling and Faster Takedowns Now in Effect

India's amended IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, officially notified February 10, 2026, are now fully operational. They mandate AI-generated content labelling across platforms, reduce harmful-content takedown windows to 3 hours, and impose real-time product-level compliance on foreign AI platforms and social media intermediaries. Global AI firms face direct legal risk for non-compliance, with India Briefing calling the rules among the most operationally demanding digital content regulations globally. (Published: 2026-02-2026, ongoing enforcement)

  5. Southeast Asia's AI adoption is no longer experimental. The ATxEnterprise signal is clear: from logistics to healthcare to manufacturing, enterprises are running AI in production—and cybersecurity infrastructure is being rebuilt around AI-native threat models.
