Coverage Date: Wednesday, July 30, 2026

Deeptech, AI & Cybersecurity news from India and Southeast Asia — all items confirmed published on 2026-07-30.

AI & Machine Learning

Indian Physical AI Attracts $155 Mn in 2026 as Robotics and Real-World Data Gain Traction

Entrackr published a data-driven report showing India's "Physical AI" segment — robotics startups and real-world training-data companies — has drawn about $155 million in funding in 2026 so far. Notable rounds include Hakimo ($12 Mn), SwitchOn ($8 Mn pre-Series B), Human Archive ($8.2 Mn seed), and Mowito ($3 Mn), alongside launches like Neocambrian AI's India-focused robotics data factory. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

FAR.AI Opens First International Office in Singapore

FAR.AI, an independent nonprofit focused on trustworthy and secure development of advanced AI, announced the opening of its first international office in Singapore. The office will serve as the organization's Asia Center of Excellence and anchor its long-term AI-safety research presence in the region. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- The Manila Times (PR Newswire)

Temus and Thinking Machines Data Science Join Forces to Scale Enterprise AI Across Southeast Asia

Temus, the Singapore digital transformation firm established by Temasek, announced a strategic investment in Philippines-headquartered Thinking Machines Data Science. The partnership pairs Temus's transformation capabilities with Thinking Machines' data science expertise to scale enterprise AI adoption across Southeast Asia. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Media OutReach Newswire

STAMPEDE Creates 11,000 Free AI-Enabled Business Pages for Singapore F&B Operators

As Singapore commits more than S$1 billion to AI research and talent development from 2025 to 2030, homegrown loyalty platform STAMPEDE announced it has created over 11,000 free, claimable AI-enabled business pages for F&B establishments across the city-state, bringing small food businesses into the national AI drive. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Media OutReach Newswire

Cybersecurity

Malaysia Arrests 335 in Online Scam Raids at Johor's Forest City

Malaysian police raided a scam operation housed in the Forest City development near Singapore, arresting 335 people including 309 Chinese nationals, 19 Indonesians, four Myanmar nationals and three Malaysians. Investigators say victims were approached through Telegram, WhatsApp and TikTok, underscoring how Southeast Asia's cyber-fraud industry keeps relocating within the region despite crackdowns. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- The Japan Times

Singapore Police Investigate 255 People Over Scam Cases Worth About $5.6 Million

The Singapore Police Force said 255 people are under investigation for suspected involvement in more than 660 scam cases — spanning e-commerce, phishing, job, government-official impersonation, investment and lucky-draw scams — with victims believed to have lost around $5.6 million. The sweep is part of Singapore's intensified anti-scam enforcement following the launch of its dedicated Cyber Command earlier in July. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- The Star

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

Drone Startup SUIND Raises Rs 20.5 Cr Seed Round Led by Transition VC

Bengaluru- and Zurich-based SUIND, founded by ETH Zurich alumni Kunal Shrivastava and Kevin Kleber, raised Rs 20.5 crore in a seed round led by Transition VC with participation from IIMA Ventures and CIIE Initiatives, valuing it near Rs 80 crore post-money. The startup builds autonomous drones that navigate via computer vision instead of GPS, and will scale its Bumblebee agri-drone while developing the WASP surveillance and infrastructure-inspection platform. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr
- Startup Story

Apna Mart Raises Rs 120 Cr in Series C Led by Accel and Fundamentum

Quick-commerce startup Apna Mart closed a Rs 120 crore Series C co-led by Accel India and Fundamentum, with participation from Peak XV Partners, roughly doubling its valuation to about Rs 1,470 crore. The franchise-led omnichannel grocery player focuses on 10-minute delivery in Tier II/III cities, competing with Blinkit, Instamart and Zepto. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

Arboreal Bioinnovations Raises Rs 230 Cr Series A Led by Edelweiss's EAAA and Omnivore

Food-ingredients and bioinnovation startup Arboreal raised Rs 230 crore in a Series A round led by Edelweiss's EAAA and agri-focused fund Omnivore — one of the larger Series A rounds in India's food-science/biotech space this year. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

Sid's Farm Raises Over Rs 81 Cr in Pre-Series B Round

Hyderabad-based D2C dairy brand Sid's Farm, founded by Dr. Kishore Indukuri, completed a pre-Series B round of over Rs 81 crore from Omnivore, NSFO, Dodla Dairy, Next Bharat Ventures and LIO. Funds will go toward supply chain, manufacturing and distribution expansion, product innovation and farmer partnerships. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

PhonePe Launches PulsePro for Enterprise Market Intelligence

Fintech major PhonePe launched PulsePro, an enterprise market-intelligence product built on its payments data, extending its consumer-facing Pulse insights platform into a commercial data-intelligence offering for businesses. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

Zepto Postpones IPO, May Raise Rs 1,000 Cr in Pre-IPO Round

Quick-commerce unicorn Zepto has postponed its planned IPO and is instead weighing a pre-IPO round of about Rs 1,000 crore — a notable shift for one of India's most-watched startup listings amid cash-runway scrutiny. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- Entrackr

Tech Policy & Regulation

Meta to Appear Before Indian Government Within 7-10 Days, Says MeitY Secretary

MeitY Secretary S Krishnan said Meta has agreed to send its senior-most representatives before the Indian government within 7-10 days to explain policy and technical issues, following the brief removal of PM Modi's Facebook video on exam paper leaks. Meta has expressed regret and set up new protocols for prominent accounts; the government wants clarity at both policy and technical levels. (Published: 2026-07-30)
- ANI News
- The Tribune
