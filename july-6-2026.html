AI, Cybersecurity & Deeptech News from India & Southeast Asia

AI and Machine Learning

PM Modi: India's Youth Will Drive AI, Robotics and Next-Gen Tech Revolution with Made-in-India Chips

Speaking around the launch of the CG Semi OSAT facility in Sanand, Gujarat, PM Narendra Modi said India's young workforce will power the global revolution in artificial intelligence, robotics and next-generation technologies using semiconductors made in India. He highlighted specialised semiconductor training for young employees — including women from remote and tribal regions trained in Malaysia — and framed the ₹7,500+ crore plant (eventual capacity ~5 billion chips/year) as feeding AI, 5G, IoT, automotive and industrial electronics. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- DD India

Cybersecurity Threats and Solutions

Mastermind of ₹500 Crore Cybercrime Syndicate Arrested in Pune

Rajasthan Police's cyber crime wing arrested Yuvraj Satish Mudaliar (35) from Pune, alleged kingpin of an investment-fraud syndicate run through a WhatsApp group called "105 IND STOCKS ADV" that lured victims nationwide with fake stock-trading profits; technical analysis pegged the fraud at roughly ₹500 crore. He allegedly ran fake finance companies, opened mule bank accounts using loan-applicant documents for a ₹10,000 commission, and laundered proceeds via ATMs, hawala and USDT purchases on Binance. His wallet and accounts have been frozen. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- ETV Bharat
- Devdiscourse

Deep Tech Startups and Investments

DealStreetAsia Weekly Wrap: NIIF's $3.5B Government Commitment, Biobot Surgical's S$20M Round Headline India–SEA Deal Flow

DealStreetAsia's "Editor's Take: The Week That Was (June 29–July 4)", published Sunday morning, rounded up regional dealmaking: India's state-backed NIIF secured an additional ₹30,000 crore (~$3.5B) commitment from the Indian government; Singapore surgical-robotics firm Biobot Surgical raised S$20M (~$15.5M) anchored by Temasek-backed ClavystBio; 3one4 Capital closed a $15M fund backed by British International Investment; Indonesia's Satu Dental raised ~$6M; and Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison completed the carve-out of its fibre unit. It also noted China holding 381 unicorns while India and SEA slipped in rankings. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- DealStreetAsia

Sanand's Big Chip Moment: 'Made in India' Semiconductors to Be Exported to Japan, US and Europe

Chips assembled and tested at the new CG Semi OSAT plant in Sanand — a JV of CG Power (Murugappa Group) with Japan's Renesas and Thailand's Stars Microelectronics — will go into cars, two-wheelers and industrial applications, and will be exported to Japan, the US and Europe, per IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. The plant starts at ~200 million chips/year with a stated scale-up ambition of 5 billion units annually under the India Semiconductor Mission. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- BusinessToday

Regional Tech Policy and Regulations

MeitY Orders Meta to Disable Instagram Ads and Content Promoting Child Sexual Abuse Material

India's IT ministry issued a notice directing Meta's Instagram to disable all advertisements and content promoting or facilitating access to CSAM, and demanded a detailed explanation within seven days; senior Meta/Instagram executives were summoned. The action follows a BBC report alleging Meta's recommendation algorithm and paid ads surfaced CSAM-linked content despite Meta's ad policies; Meta responded that it uses AI to proactively detect violating content among its 3.5 billion users. This extends a week of Indian government pressure on messaging and social platforms. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- Business Standard
- The Week

Notable Tech Industry Events

CG Semi OSAT Inauguration: India's First End-to-End Chip Assembly-and-Test Facility Opens in Sanand

PM Modi inaugurated the ₹7,500–7,600 crore CG Semi OSAT facility at GIDC Sanand — one of the first four India Semiconductor Mission projects and India's first end-to-end OSAT plant. Modi called it a model of Indo-Japanese-Thai cooperation, cited Silicon Valley/Hsinchu/Tsukuba-style cluster ambitions, and said output would scale from 20 crore to 500 crore units annually. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- The Researchers
- PIB press release

PM Modi Reveals He Drew Up a Semiconductor Plant Plan for Gujarat 20 Years Ago

In remarks at the Sanand event, Modi disclosed that over two decades ago he had identified 350–400 acres near Gandhinagar for a chip plant that never took off, tracing the arc to the December 2021 approval of India Semiconductor Mission 1.0 (₹76,000 crore outlay). As of December 2025, 10 semiconductor projects across six states have been approved with ~₹1.6 lakh crore of investment. (Published: 2026-07-05)
- BusinessToday

Key Takeaways

  1. Semiconductors dominated the weekend narrative. The CG Semi OSAT inauguration in Sanand — India's first end-to-end assembly-and-test plant, built with Japanese and Thai partners — was the single biggest story, positioned explicitly as the hardware foundation for India's AI and deeptech ambitions, with exports to Japan, the US and Europe planned.

  2. Government capital is flowing at scale. NIIF's fresh ~$3.5B commitment from the Indian government and 3one4 Capital's BII-backed $15M fund signal continued state and DFI participation in India's venture ecosystem, even as India and SEA slip in global unicorn rankings versus China.

  3. Cyber-enabled financial fraud remains India's dominant threat pattern. The ₹500 crore WhatsApp investment-fraud bust — with mule accounts, hawala and USDT laundering — underscores that large-scale social-engineering fraud, not sophisticated malware, is the region's most damaging cyber threat.

  4. Platform accountability pressure is intensifying. MeitY's CSAM notice to Meta, with a seven-day deadline and executive summons, continues the Indian government's escalating enforcement posture toward global platforms.

  5. Sunday effect: overall news volume was low; no confirmed SEA-specific cybersecurity or policy items surfaced for July 5.
