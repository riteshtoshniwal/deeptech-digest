Cybersecurity Threats & Solutions

Microsoft June 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes 198 flaws, including 3 zero-days

Microsoft's June 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 198 vulnerabilities, among them three actively exploited or publicly disclosed zero-days, 33 critical flaws and 55 additional remote-code-execution bugs. Standouts include a wormable Windows Kernel RCE (CVE-2026-45657, CVSS 9.8) and a BitLocker security-feature bypass (CVE-2026-50507). Enterprises across India and SEA are prioritising patch rollout. (Published: 2026-06-09, recent context)

CERT-In mandates 12-hour patching for internet-facing flaws amid AI-assisted attacks

India's CERT-In has issued guidance recommending a 12-hour patching window for critical internet-facing vulnerabilities, citing the acceleration of AI-assisted exploitation. The directive raises the operational bar for Indian enterprises, financial institutions and government platforms. (Published: 2026-05, recent context)
- The Hacker News

Cybersecurity ranked India's #1 national risk for 2026

The World Economic Forum's Global Risk Report 2026 ranks cybersecurity as India's top national risk, ahead of economic downturn, climate disasters and armed conflict. India recorded more than 2.2 million cybersecurity incidents between 2021 and mid-2025 — averaging over 3,000 attacks a day — with financial services, healthcare, telecom and government most affected. (Published: 2026, recent context)
