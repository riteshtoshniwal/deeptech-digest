AI & Machine Learning

Indosat launches AI infrastructure venture Zankore with Ooredoo, NVIDIA and Nokia

Indonesian telco Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison teamed up with Ooredoo Group, NVIDIA and Nokia to launch Zankore, an AI infrastructure platform targeting Southeast Asia. It aims to build up to 1 GW of NVIDIA DSX AI Factory capacity, with a first phase of roughly 200 MW using GB300 NVL72 systems expected in H1 2027. Former Ericsson CTO Ulf Ewaldsson has been named CEO. (Published: 2026-08-06)

Acrab, a Singapore-headquartered AI infrastructure startup building a full-stack platform combining custom silicon, edge AI models and software orchestration for real-time AI agents, raised $130 million from Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India and Vertex Growth plus new investors. Cumulative funding now exceeds $480 million, and the company expects first revenue this year from industrial deployments of its GΞLIX 1-powered Agent Box. (Published: 2026-08-06)

Lok Sabha passes Taxation Laws Amendment Bill, clearing path to levy MDR on UPI payments

India's lower house passed the Taxation Laws (Amendment) Bill, opening the door for a merchant discount rate (MDR) on UPI payments — a significant potential shift for India's zero-MDR digital payments regime. (Published: 2026-08-06)

Gains from unexercised ESOP buybacks taxable as capital gains: ITAT

Key Takeaways

  1. AI infrastructure is the megatrend in SEA capital flows. Two of the day's biggest stories — Indosat's Zankore venture (up to 1 GW of NVIDIA AI factory capacity) and Acrab's $130M Series B — show Southeast Asia racing to build sovereign AI compute, with telcos and chip-adjacent startups leading.

  2. India's deeptech exit pipeline is maturing. Garuda Aerospace's SEBI nod and Shiprocket's IPO push (even at a trimmed valuation) signal that dronetech and logistics-tech are reaching public markets, while Piper Serica's Bharat Tech Fund first close adds fresh domestic growth capital.

The Lok Sabha clearing a path to MDR on UPI is potentially the most consequential regulatory shift of the day — it could reshape unit economics for the entire payments ecosystem. The ITAT ESOP ruling and scrutiny of Google's $15B data centre plan add to a busy regulatory day.
