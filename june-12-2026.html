AI, Cybersecurity & Deeptech News from India & Southeast Asia

Daily Deeptech & AI Digest

Coverage date: June 11, 2026 (news from the previous calendar day)

A roundup of AI, cybersecurity, and deeptech developments across India and Southeast Asia, with portfolio tracking for ThinKuvate.

AI & Machine Learning

India to be VivaTech 2026's Official AI Partner Country

India will headline VivaTech 2026 (opening June 17 in Paris) as the Official AI Partner Country, with participation coordinated by the India Trade Promotion Organisation alongside MeitY, the Ministry of Commerce, DPIIT, and the Ministry of Education. The India Pavilion will convene government officials, investors, and startups around AI partnerships, investment, and technology exports. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- TechRepublic

Indian filmmakers lean into generative AI as Hollywood debates it

While Hollywood remains divided over generative AI, Indian content-production firms are adopting it aggressively. CNBC reported one company that shot footage with human actors and then used AI to generate animated characters, cutting costs from millions of dollars to a few hundred and compressing production timelines from roughly six months to weeks. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- CNBC

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

Ethereal Machines raises $28.5M Series B for precision manufacturing

Bengaluru-based deeptech startup Ethereal Machines raised $28.5 million in a Series B round led by Avataar Ventures, with participation from Peak XV Partners and others. The capital will expand its manufacturing facility, strengthen its software stack, build capabilities for the semiconductor sector, and support global expansion. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- YourStory

Dream Aerospace raises ₹10 Cr pre-Series A for in-space propulsion

Space-propulsion startup Dream Aerospace Technologies raised ₹10 crore in a pre-Series A round led by 247VC, with Campus Angels Network and Chandigarh Angels Network participating. Funds will complete flight qualification of its ATOM thruster series and CUBEHOOD propulsion module, expand its IIT Kanpur facility, and support a first in-orbit demonstration in 2026. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- YourStory

TakeMe2Space and Astrobase win IN-SPACe Technology Adoption Fund support

Deeptech startup TakeMe2Space secured IN-SPACe Technology Adoption Fund backing to build StarSense, an indigenous AI-powered star tracker that aims to cut India's reliance on imported satellite-orientation sensors. Separately, Astrobase was selected under the same fund to develop an indigenous 80-tonne full-flow staged-combustion LOX-methane rocket engine, currently in hot-fire testing in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- YourStory

Biodimension raises ₹8 Cr for life sciences

Bengaluru-based life-sciences startup Biodimension raised ₹8 crore in a round led by IAN Angel Fund, with participation from Campus Angels Network and angel investors including Dr. Sampath Srisailam. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- YourStory

Cybersecurity

India admitted to Anthropic's Project Glasswing cybersecurity preview

India was named among a select group of nations granted access to Anthropic's advanced cybersecurity AI model preview under Project Glasswing, the company's cybersecurity initiative now spanning roughly 150 organizations across more than 15 countries. Participants in the program have collectively identified over 10,000 high- or critical-severity software vulnerabilities using the model. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- CRN Asia

India cyber risk remains elevated amid fresh fintech and financial-sector breaches

India continues to face heavy breach pressure: recently disclosed incidents include HDFC Mutual Fund (attributed to threat actor MORPHEUS), fintech M1xchange, and Apollo Pipes (both attributed to WorldLeaks). The World Economic Forum's Global Risk Report 2026 ranks cybersecurity as India's number-one national risk, and CERT-In data shows more than 2.2 million incidents recorded between 2021 and mid-2025. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- Breachsense
- Seqrite Threat Insights

Southeast Asia Tech

SuperAI Singapore 2026 wraps with Korean travel-tech showcase

SuperAI Singapore 2026 concluded its run at Marina Bay Sands on June 10–11, drawing roughly 10,000 attendees from more than 1,500 AI companies. On June 11, the KTSC Travel Tech Startup Showcase featured eight Korean startups building solutions across hospitality, MICE, AI-powered travel, and next-generation guest services. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- Tech Singapore Advocates

Ransomware pressure persists across Southeast Asia

Security researchers continue to flag Southeast Asia as a heavily targeted ransomware region, with Indonesia, the Philippines, and Thailand recording the highest volumes of blocked attacks. Fragmented regulation, constrained security budgets, and uneven infrastructure protection keep the region a frequent target for cybercriminals. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- SecurityBrief Asia

Regional Tech Policy & Regulation

MeitY continues phased rollout of DPDP Rules 2025

India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology continues to fine-tune implementing regulations for the Digital Personal Data Protection framework. Following notification of the DPDP Rules 2025 in November 2025, enforcement is proceeding in phases — early rules are already in force, with the bulk of provisions (including consent, data-fiduciary obligations, and breach-notification mechanics) phasing in over the next 18 months. Penalties run up to ₹250 crore. (Published: 2026-06-11)
- EY India
- PwC

Key Takeaways

  • Deeptech and spacetech dominated India's funding flow on June 11, with Ethereal Machines' $28.5M Series B, Dream Aerospace's pre-Series A, and dual IN-SPACe Technology Adoption Fund awards to TakeMe2Space and Astrobase signaling sustained investor and government appetite for hard-tech and indigenous space capability.
  • Healthcare-fintech is emerging as a distinct vertical, underscored by QubeHealth-Pay's Series A at a ₹416 Cr valuation (see portfolio section).
  • AI adoption is accelerating in India's creative economy, with generative AI sharply cutting film/animation production costs and timelines.
  • Cybersecurity remains India's top systemic risk, but the country is also being woven into global AI-security efforts via Anthropic's Project Glasswing preview.
  • Southeast Asia's AI story is maturing from experimentation toward enterprise deployment, even as ransomware pressure on the region persists.
