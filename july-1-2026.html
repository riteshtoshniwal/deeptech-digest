Cybersecurity Threats & Solutions

Mustang Panda escalates espionage against India's hydropower & government sectors

Acronis Threat Research Unit detailed two Mustang Panda (China-nexus) espionage campaigns targeting India's hydropower sector and government organisations. The actor deployed new ZOHOMURK and MINIRECON implants via spear-phishing archives, DLL sideloading and a new SHARDLOADER loader, with ZOHOMURK abusing Zoho WorkDrive for command-and-control. (Published: 2026-06-30)
- Daily Cybersecurity Briefing — 30 June 2026
- The Hacker News

Critical libssh2 flaw (CVE-2026-55200) enables remote code execution

A critical vulnerability in libssh2 lets a malicious server trigger memory corruption and code execution on connecting clients. It affects versions up to 1.11.1 and carries a CVSS score of 9.2. A proof-of-concept has been released, though no in-the-wild exploitation has been reported yet. (Published: 2026-06-30)
- Daily Cybersecurity Briefing — 30 June 2026

"The Gentlemen" ransomware group expands its toolkit

The ransomware-as-a-service crew known as The Gentlemen added a custom Go backdoor for remote command execution and SOCKS proxying, leaning on BYOVD (bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver) techniques, DLL sideloading and PsExec for lateral movement against corporations and critical infrastructure. (Published: 2026-06-30)
- Daily Cybersecurity Briefing — 30 June 2026

INTERPOL flags a "dramatic increase" in cybercrime across Asia & the South Pacific

A new INTERPOL assessment reported a sharp rise in cybercrime across Asia and the South Pacific, driven by rapid digitalisation, rising internet penetration, new technologies, organised criminal networks and uneven cybersecurity maturity across the region. (Published: 2026-06-30)
- Daily Cybersecurity Briefing — 30 June 2026

AI & Machine Learning

India's sovereign-AI push confronts its trust problem

India's AI ambitions are moving past model-building and investment toward a harder question: can the country scale AI while building public trust? With no standalone AI law, India relies on existing statutes, sectoral oversight and emerging governance frameworks — even as deepfakes, synthetic media and public-sector AI deployment raise pressure for stronger safeguards. Analysts argue the next phase of governance may arrive through procurement standards rather than legislation. (Published: 2026-06-29)
- Business Standard

Regional Tech Policy & Regulation

India's IT Amendment Rules 2026 continue to reshape platform accountability

India's IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026 — in force since February — remain the dominant regulatory storyline, mandating a three-hour takedown window for flagged content and compulsory labelling of AI-generated "synthetic content." Ongoing commentary this week examined the compliance burden on platforms and the creator economy, and the prospect of government advisories becoming legally binding. (Published: 2026-06-29)
- Outlook Business
- Chambers and Partners

Key Takeaways & Insights

  • India is now a first-tier target for state-nexus cyber-espionage. The Mustang Panda campaigns against hydropower and government confirm critical-infrastructure targeting with novel loaders and living-off-the-cloud C2 (Zoho WorkDrive) — patch discipline and phishing-resilience are the immediate priorities.
  • A fresh critical CVE (libssh2, 9.2) with public PoC means SSH client fleets across the region should prioritise patching before exploitation appears in the wild.
  • AI funding keeps concentrating at the top. Sarvam's unicorn round and CRED's mega-raise show capital pooling into sovereign-AI and fintech, while seed activity stays broad but small-ticket.
  • Governance-by-procurement is emerging as India's pragmatic answer to the absence of a standalone AI law.
  • SEA's "flight to quality" continues — deep tech and B2B are absorbing capital that once went to consumer growth stories.
