AI, Cybersecurity & Deeptech News from India & Southeast Asia

Coverage Date: Tuesday, July 7, 2026

AI and Machine Learning

Google unveils AI-powered video commerce tools for Southeast Asia

At Google Marketing Live Southeast Asia in Singapore, Google announced new AI-driven advertising and video commerce solutions, including a Commerce Media Suite piloted with Shopee, as YouTube shopping videos in the region passed 6 million. Google said video commerce now accounts for 25% of the region's e-commerce GMV, and that users of AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search report faster (82%) and more confident (80%) purchase decisions. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- TechNode Global

Scaler launches Forward Deployed Engineer specialisation, plans to train 10,000 engineers

AI-native tech education platform Scaler launched a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) specialisation, committing Rs 25 crore to train 10,000 engineers who can bridge AI models with enterprise deployment. The move responds to a reported 729% year-on-year global surge in FDE hiring at companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, and Databricks. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- YourStory

Thailand's Customs Department deploys AI to stop drug transit through airports

Thailand's Customs Department is stepping up outbound passenger and cargo screening with AI, X-ray machines, K-9 units, and risk-management systems, after trafficking cases raised concerns that transnational drug networks are exploiting the country's airports and logistics infrastructure. The story led BERNAMA's ASEAN headlines roundup for the day. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- BERNAMA

ORF: US AI access controls signal the rise of a "digital thalassocracy"

New Delhi-based Observer Research Foundation published an analysis arguing that recent US restrictions on access to frontier AI models mark a shift from controlling hardware to controlling AI capabilities themselves — a modern analogue of maritime chokepoint power. The piece warns of a "dependence paradox" in which restrictions accelerate self-reliance efforts in India, China, and elsewhere. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- Observer Research Foundation

Cybersecurity Threats and Solutions

Malaysia reviews cybersecurity laws, weighs whipping for offenders and victim compensation

The Malaysian government announced it is reviewing its cybersecurity laws, including the possibility of introducing whipping as a punishment for cybercrime offenders and mechanisms for compensating victims — including potential refunds from financial institutions. Current laws punish perpetrators mainly with fines and imprisonment, without adequate provisions for victim remedies. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- New Straits Times

Philippine central bank urges banks to prepare for frontier AI cyber risks

The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas warned supervised financial institutions that frontier AI systems capable of finding software vulnerabilities, generating exploit pathways, and launching multi-stage attacks with little human intervention pose a growing threat. It urged banks to maintain updated inventories of internet-facing assets, strengthen authentication, deploy AI-powered security tools, and update business continuity plans. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- Philippine Daily Inquirer

Veeam partners with DSCI to strengthen India's cyber resilience and DPDP readiness

Veeam signed a strategic MoU with the Data Security Council of India (DSCI) to strengthen India's cyber resilience ecosystem through awareness, capability building, compliance enablement, and policy collaboration ahead of DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) deadlines. The tie-up connects to Veeam's "Bharat Cyber Suraksha – Saksham Bharat" mission, which aims to skill over 100,000 professionals in data protection and ransomware recovery. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- Express Computer

Sattrix Information Security wins Rs 9.30 crore cybersecurity order from State Bank of India

Indian cybersecurity firm Sattrix Information Security received a Letter of Intent from State Bank of India for a three-year, Rs 9.30 crore managed security services contract, under which it will act as an MSSP for SBI's Cyber Security Defence Centre — monitoring systems, detecting threats, and responding to cyber risks. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- Business Standard

Cybersecurity vendor Cyn.Ai plans India entry, seeks MSSPs and specialist partners

Cybersecurity company Cyn.Ai announced plans to enter the Indian market, launching with just five to eight partners and offering resale, co-sell, and white-label engagement models backed by certification, local pre-sales support, and higher margins for delivery-led partners. The company is specifically courting MSSPs and specialist cybersecurity channel partners. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- CRN Asia

Deep Tech Startups and Investments

Defence-maritime AI startup Blurgs AI raises $2.2M

IIT-Madras alumni-founded Blurgs AI raised $2.2 million led by Pravega Ventures and Shastra VC to expand its AI-powered intelligence platforms for defence, national security, and commercial maritime applications globally. The Chennai- and Bengaluru-based startup counts the Indian Navy, Indian Coast Guard, Bharat Electronics, DRDO labs, and Mumbai Port Authority among its customers. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- YourStory

Physical AI startup Mowito raises $3M pre-seed for industrial robotics foundation models

Bengaluru-based Mowito, which builds foundation models for industrial robotic arms, raised $3 million in a pre-seed round led by Version One Ventures, with participation from All In Capital, Unisol, iSeed, and angels including Soumith Chintala of Thinking Machines Lab. The capital will fund US market expansion, engineering and GTM hiring, and deployments with automotive and electronics manufacturers. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- YourStory

Econovus Packaging raises Rs 40 Cr pre-Series A led by Zerodha's Rainmatter

Pune-based sustainable packaging startup Econovus raised Rs 40 crore led by Rainmatter by Zerodha with participation from Rockstud Capital — its first institutional round after being bootstrapped and profitable since 2019. The company builds IP-backed materials and design-to-cost packaging, including UN-certified lithium-ion battery packaging and automotive packaging. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- YourStory

B Capital closes $500M early-stage Ascent Fund III at hard cap

US- and Singapore-based B Capital closed its third dedicated early-stage fund at its $500 million hard cap, nearly double its $254 million predecessor. The fund targets seed-to-Series B startups in AI, enterprise, healthcare, energy, and frontier tech across North America and Asia; the firm now manages over $12 billion. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- DealStreetAsia

Regional Tech Policy and Regulations

RBI tells parliamentary panel crypto should not be legalised, pitches containment

The Reserve Bank of India told the Parliamentary Standing Committee on Finance that virtual digital assets threaten the economy and should not be legalised, pitching a containment strategy leaning towards prohibition to insulate banks from the asset class. The RBI also criticised stablecoins as undermining monetary sovereignty and pushed the digital rupee (CBDC) as the alternative, even as India counts 39.3 million verified crypto users. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- MediaNama

TRAI seeks IT Act powers to act against Truecaller and other call management apps

India's telecom regulator TRAI is seeking designation as an "authorised agency" under the IT Act so it can formally act against third-party call management apps like Truecaller, Hiya, and Whoscall, which it says wrongly tag or block calls from the 1400/1600 commercial numbering series as spam. MeitY has reportedly agreed; industry body IAMAI argues TRAI is exceeding its statutory remit and that the move threatens intermediaries' Section 79 safe harbour. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- MediaNama

MeitY's messaging-app username freeze heads toward July 9 deadline

MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed on July 7 that the government views username-based communication on WhatsApp, Telegram, and Signal as a serious cybercrime facilitator, ahead of Meta's July 9 deadline to justify WhatsApp's paused username feature. The Internet Freedom Foundation and legal experts argue no provision of the IT Act authorises pre-approval or blocking of a platform feature; Zoho-backed Arattai has already disabled its username feature preemptively. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- Tech Times

Info Edge takes 100% control of Coding Ninjas for ~Rs 40 Cr

Naukri parent Info Edge announced it will acquire the remaining stake (~45-49%) in Sunrise Mentors (Coding Ninjas) from the founders for about Rs 39.9 crore, making the edtech firm a wholly owned subsidiary. The deal, expected to close within 45 days, aims to deepen synergies between Naukri and Coding Ninjas, including mini AI course offerings for Naukri users. (Published: 2026-07-07)

- Entrackr

M-DAQ Global enters Vietnam; ESR–Mitsubishi launch $80M Indonesia logistics build-out

Singapore fintech M-DAQ Global signed definitive agreements to integrate METech, majority shareholder of Vietnamese licensed payments provider PayME — its first direct presence in Vietnam and fifth regulated licence across ASEAN. Separately, ESR and Mitsubishi's MCUDI announced joint development of two Grade-A logistics and industrial facilities in Greater Jakarta worth over $80 million. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- DealStreetAsia

Pocket FM appoints Abhilash Padival as CFO amid global expansion push

Audio series platform Pocket FM named Bank of America veteran Abhilash Padival as Chief Financial Officer to oversee global financial strategy, capital allocation, investor relations, and governance as it expands internationally and invests in AI-driven storytelling. (Published: 2026-07-07)
- YourStory

Key Takeaways

  1. Defence and physical AI are pulling Indian deeptech capital. Two of the day's three India deeptech rounds — Blurgs AI ($2.2M, maritime/defence intelligence) and Mowito ($3M, industrial robotics foundation models) — sit squarely in the defence/physical-AI theme, both backed by early customers before institutional capital.

  2. Regulators across the region are converging on AI-era cyber risk. The Philippine central bank explicitly warned banks about frontier AI-driven attacks, Malaysia is rewriting cybercrime law with victim compensation in scope, and India's DSCI-Veeam MoU ties cyber resilience directly to DPDP compliance — a notable single-day cluster of AI-security policymaking.

  3. India's platform-regulation friction is intensifying. TRAI's bid for IT Act powers over caller-ID apps and MeitY's July 9 ultimatum on messaging usernames both stretch existing statutory authority, drawing pushback from IAMAI and the Internet Freedom Foundation — signalling a contested second half of 2026 for intermediary regulation.

  4. SEA commerce infrastructure keeps compounding. Google's video-commerce push (25% of SEA e-commerce GMV), M-DAQ's Vietnam entry, and the ESR-Mitsubishi $80M Jakarta logistics build-out all point to sustained infrastructure investment behind Southeast Asian digital commerce.
