AI & Machine Learning

Sarvam becomes India's newest AI unicorn with $234M Series B first close

Bengaluru-based AI startup Sarvam raised $234 million in the first close of a $300 million Series B round at a post-money valuation of $1.5 billion, vaulting it into India's AI unicorn club. The raise was the largest growth-stage deal of the week and underscores investor appetite for India's sovereign foundation-model players. AI startups led the country's weekly funding charts with six deals. (Published: 2026-06-20)

Pramaana Labs raises $27M seed for AI verification and accountability platform

AI verification and accountability platform Pramaana Labs closed a $27 million seed round led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from Accel, Boldcap, Nexus Venture Partners, Premji Invest and Unbound. It was the largest early-stage deal of the week, signalling rising investor focus on AI trust, provenance and audit infrastructure. (Published: 2026-06-20)

Cybersecurity

Texas Parks & Wildlife discloses third-party data breach

Texas Cyber Command disclosed a third-party data breach affecting the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department, exposing personal records of affected individuals. The incident, tied to a compromised external vendor, was made public on June 20 and adds to a run of supply-chain-driven breaches in the public sector. (Published: 2026-06-20)
- The Hacker News
- SecurityWeek

FortiBleed campaign: ~86,600 FortiGate devices compromised, CISA advisory active

CISA continued urging Fortinet customers to harden internet-exposed FortiGate appliances against an ongoing campaign codenamed "FortiBleed," attributed to Russian-speaking threat actors. As of June 19–20, roughly 86,644 devices had been compromised, with the advisory remaining active through the weekend. (Published: 2026-06-19, ongoing through 2026-06-20)
- The Hacker News
- ReconShield — June 2026 Cybersecurity Review

Active exploitation of Gravity SMTP WordPress flaw (CVE-2026-4020)

Threat actors are actively exploiting CVE-2026-4020, a medium-severity information-disclosure flaw in the Gravity SMTP WordPress plugin installed on roughly 100,000 sites. Unauthenticated attackers can extract configuration data, API keys, secrets and OAuth tokens, making prompt patching critical for site operators. (Published: 2026-06-20)
- The Hacker News — Vulnerability
- ReconShield — June 2026 Cybersecurity Review

Indian startups raise ~$432M in the week ending June 20; funding jumps 70%

A week-in-review published June 20 showed 23 Indian startups raised nearly $432 million across 21 disclosed deals (7 growth-stage, 14 early-stage), up 70.5% week-on-week. YourNest closes Rs 400 Cr deeptech continuation fund

Deeptech VC YourNest Venture Capital closed YourNest Continuum Fund I, a Rs 400 crore continuation vehicle anchored by HDFC AMC Select Fund of Funds I. The fund will deploy Rs 60–90 crore across seven portfolio companies including Miko, Dozee, Exponent Energy, Twid, Opkey and Thriwe. (Published: 2026-06-20)

Southeast Asia Tech

Singapore dominates Southeast Asia's AI funding; ~680 AI startups raise $2.3B

A regional report highlighted that around 680 AI startups attracted more than US$2.3 billion across Southeast Asia in the 12 months to June 2026 — about 32% of regional private funding. Regional Tech Policy & Regulation

India blocks Telegram until June 22 ahead of NEET re-exam

Ahead of the NEET (UG) 2026 re-examination on June 21, the government temporarily blocked Telegram in India until June 22 and directed the platform to disable its message-editing feature until June 30, citing fraud and misinformation. The action — coordinated by NTA, I4C, MeitY and state police — followed channels falsely claiming to sell the re-exam paper, and remained in force through June 20. (Published: 2026-06-20)
- Entrackr

ARIFAC launched as national AML/CFT industry platform

ARIFAC, a public-private anti-money-laundering and counter-terror-financing platform, was launched with FIU-India as observer and PCI-FCC as secretariat. It brings together regulated entities across financial sectors to strengthen compliance, training, intelligence-sharing and capability-building against financial crime. (Published: 2026-06-20)
- Entrackr — Weekly Funding Report (June 15–20)
- ET BFSI

Notable Tech Industry Events

Jio Platforms files DRHP for fresh-issue-only IPO; NSE files for mega OFS

Reliance's digital arm Jio Platforms filed its draft prospectus with SEBI for an IPO comprising a fresh issue of up to 27 crore shares, with reports estimating a Rs 30,000–40,000 crore raise at a Rs 10–15 lakh crore valuation. Separately, the National Stock Exchange filed its long-awaited DRHP, structured entirely as an offer for sale of 14.89 crore shares. (Published: 2026-06-20)

Key Takeaways

  • India's AI funding momentum is intact. Sarvam's $1.5B unicorn round plus Pramaana Labs' $27M seed show capital flowing to both foundation-model builders and AI trust/governance infrastructure. AI led the week with six deals.
  • Weekly funding surged 70% to ~$432M, with cleantech (SolarSquare) and healthtech (Vetic, HealthQuad) joining AI as magnets for growth capital — a sign of breadth, not just AI concentration.
  • Deeptech capital is deepening. YourNest's Rs 400 Cr continuation fund signals maturing exit/liquidity mechanics for Indian deeptech portfolios.
  • Cybersecurity risk stays elevated. A fresh public-sector breach (Texas Parks & Wildlife), the active FortiBleed campaign on ~86K FortiGate devices, and live WordPress exploitation reinforce supply-chain and edge-device exposure.
  • Policy is hands-on. India's pre-exam Telegram block and the ARIFAC AML/CFT launch show regulators acting tactically on misinformation and financial-crime fronts.
  • Singapore remains SEA's AI gravity well, capturing the lion's share of the region's $2.3B AI funding over the past year.
