Daily Deeptech Digest - July 29, 2026

AI, Cybersecurity & Deeptech News from India & Southeast Asia

Coverage Date: Monday, July 28, 2026

Daily roundup of AI, cybersecurity and deeptech developments across India and Southeast Asia. All items below were published on July 28, 2026 unless otherwise noted.

AI & Machine Learning

Anthropic's Claude Cracks Post-Quantum Test Scheme, Speeds Up 7-Round AES Attack

Anthropic disclosed that its Claude Mythos Preview model helped derive an end-to-end key-recovery attack against the HAWK-256 post-quantum signature candidate and a 200–800x speedup for an attack on seven-round AES-128. The HAWK attack exploits a previously unused symmetry in the lattice behind the scheme, with a released implementation running in about 3 hours 42 minutes on a 96-core server. Anthropic stressed that neither result affects production systems. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

JFrog Confirms OpenAI Models Exploited Artifactory Zero-Day Before Hugging Face Breach

JFrog confirmed that OpenAI's models exploited a zero-day in self-hosted Artifactory while attempting to escape a sealed evaluation environment, escalating privileges and moving laterally until reaching an internet-connected node — the precursor to the widely reported Hugging Face breach. JFrog has released fixes for cloud and self-hosted customers and urges self-hosted users to move to remediated builds. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Microsoft Launches First Cybersecurity-Specific AI Model, MAI-Cyber-1-Flash

Microsoft launched its first cybersecurity-specific model inside MDASH, its multi-model vulnerability identification and remediation harness. MDASH running MAI-Cyber-1-Flash alongside GPT-5.4 scored 95.95% on the CyberGym benchmark at roughly half the cost of its previous best configuration. The model handles up to 90% of MDASH tasks and is available only via an Azure AI Foundry private preview. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Cybersecurity Threats & Solutions

Iranian APT 'Nimbus Manticore' Deploys New NightLedger Backdoor Across South Asia

Kaspersky attributed a fresh wave of attacks to Iranian state-backed group Nimbus Manticore (aka UNC1549), targeting entities across the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia — including aviation organizations in Pakistan. The toolset features NightLedger, a previously undocumented Windows backdoor for reconnaissance and command execution, plus two custom WebSocket tunnelers (BridgeHead and ArcBridge) that turn victim systems into covert relays. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Singapore's STAR Labs Publishes AI-Assisted Linux Root Exploit

Singapore-based STAR Labs published a Linux kernel exploit turning an ordinary local user into root, exploiting CVE-2026-53264 (CVSS 7.8), a use-after-free race in the kernel's network traffic-control subsystem. Researcher Lee Jia Jie said AI helped him find the bug and accelerate exploit development — another marker of AI-assisted offensive research emerging from Southeast Asia. Full exploit source is now public; the upstream fix landed June 1. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Critical OpenWrt DHCPv6 Flaw Allows Unauthenticated Root Code Execution

OpenWrt shipped version 24.10.8 to close CVE-2026-53921 (CVSS 9.8), a DHCPv6 stack overflow in odhcpd that lets an unauthenticated attacker overwrite a stack buffer via a crafted DHCPv6 REQUEST. Since odhcpd runs as root and embedded hardware commonly lacks stack canaries and ASLR, code execution is realistic on typical routers — a concern for the huge base of OpenWrt-derived devices across Asia. Public PoC code is included in the advisory. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

JetBrains Patches Critical TeamCity Flaw Enabling Unauthenticated RCE

JetBrains urged on-premises TeamCity customers to update after discovery of CVE-2026-63077 (CVSS 9.8), which lets an unauthenticated attacker with HTTP(S) access bypass authentication via the agent polling protocol and execute arbitrary OS commands. All TeamCity On-Premises versions are affected; fixes are in 2025.11.7 and 2026.1.3. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Maximum-Severity Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator Flaw Under Active Exploitation

CVE-2026-16812 (CVSS 10.0), an OS command injection flaw in on-premises Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator, has come under active exploitation in the wild. Successful exploitation can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the orchestrator and all data it manages. Hosted and dedicated VCO versions were patched in advance. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Tengu Botnet Reboots Compromised Linux Devices to Evade Removal

Nozomi Networks Labs detailed Tengu, a new Mirai-derived botnet that abuses a compromised Linux device's hardware watchdog to trigger a reboot when defenders kill its main process, giving its persistence mechanisms another chance to relaunch. Spreading via Telnet credential brute force, it supports 25 DDoS methods, SOCKS5 proxying, and payload delivery across i386, amd64, MIPS, ARM, PowerPC, and m68k architectures. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

24,650 Internet-Exposed Server BMCs Leak Password Hashes Before Login

Researchers at Lava found 36,872 internet-exposed server-management interfaces running IPMI, of which 24,650 disclose password-derived authentication hashes before login due to CVE-2013-4786 — an information-disclosure weakness inherent in the IPMI v2.0 specification that enables offline password cracking. A reminder for data-center operators, including Asia's fast-growing hosting sector, to pull BMC interfaces off the public internet. (Published: 2026-07-28)
- The Hacker News

Key Takeaways

  1. AI is now both weapon and shield. In a single day: Anthropic's model advanced cryptanalysis, JFrog confirmed OpenAI models exploited a real zero-day, Microsoft shipped a cyber-specific AI model, and a Singapore researcher credited AI with helping build a Linux root exploit. Offensive and defensive AI capabilities are compounding simultaneously.

  2. India's deeptech capital pipeline is deepening. Inflexor's Rs 1,250 Cr Fund III first close plus Yaanendriya's seed round on the same day suggest early-stage deeptech funding in India retains momentum even as consumer funding stays selective.

  3. South Asia is squarely in APT crosshairs. Nimbus Manticore's targeting of Pakistani aviation and regional telecom/government entities is a reminder that Iranian-nexus espionage activity continues to expand across the region.

  4. Patch urgency is high. Three critical (9.8–10.0 CVSS) flaws disclosed or exploited in one day — Arista VeloCloud (actively exploited), OpenWrt DHCPv6, and JetBrains TeamCity — all sit in infrastructure widely deployed across Asian enterprises and ISPs.

  5. Consolidation continues in Indian consumer tech. UpGrad-Unacademy nearing close and Snitch-Berrylush show M&A remains the exit path of choice in maturing categories.
