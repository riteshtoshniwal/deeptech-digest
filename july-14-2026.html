AI and Machine Learning

TCS to Build Up to 8,900-Strong 'Forward-Deployed' AI Engineering Force, Eyes AI Acquisitions

Tata Consultancy Services CEO K Krithivasan said the company plans to grow forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) — who embed with clients to accelerate AI adoption — to 1–1.5% of its workforce, roughly 5,900–8,900 people based on end-June headcount. TCS is also evaluating acquisitions in AI, data security and cybersecurity after years of shunning M&A, as annualised AI revenue growth slowed to 13% in Q1 from 28% the prior quarter. Singapore-based startup Agnes AI announced a new model release, Agnes-2.5-Flash, teased a flagship Pro model, and launched a desktop coding app, Agnes Code, positioning itself in the increasingly crowded AI coding-assistant market in Southeast Asia. (Published: 2026-07-13)

Cybersecurity Threats and Solutions

MeitY Body Tells Indian Ministries to Hold Off on Deploying OpenAI, Anthropic Cybersecurity Models

A department under India's Ministry of Electronics and IT circulated an office memorandum asking ministries not to deploy OpenAI and Anthropic frontier models for cybersecurity functions (vulnerability discovery, code review, incident triage, agentic deployment) "for now," after both companies pitched ministries directly — the Finance Ministry had specifically asked about deploying GPT-5.5 for cybersecurity work. The objection is framed as timing-related rather than a permanent bar, and lands amid India's sovereign-AI push (IndiaAI Mission, Sarvam AI, BharatGen) and an unsettled framework for government AI procurement. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- ThePrint

RedHook Android Trojan Expands From Vietnam to Indonesia

The Hacker News' weekly recap (July 13) highlighted Group-IB findings that the RedHook Android RAT — which abuses ADB Wireless Debugging for shell-level access and is distributed via spoofed government and financial websites — has expanded targeting beyond Vietnam to Indonesian users, signalling a broader Southeast Asia campaign, with payloads hosted on AWS S3 and GitHub. The recap also covered the SHELLSTORM web-shell operation (1.4M domains targeted) and the urgent Progress ShareFile shutdown advisory. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- The Hacker News

Regional Tech Policy and Regulations

TRAI Issues Clarification on Designated 1600/140 Promotional and Transactional Number Series

India's telecom regulator clarified that the 1600xx series is reserved for service/transactional calls by RBI-, SEBI-, IRDAI- and PFRDA-regulated entities and government-to-citizen communication, while 140xx is exclusively for promotional calls; 1600-series calls cannot be tagged, blocked or filtered. The clarification comes amid TRAI's push for IT Act powers against call-tagging apps such as Truecaller, Hiya and Whoscall, and IAMAI's opposition to proposed TCCCPR amendments. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- MediaNama

Andhra Pradesh Announces Social Media Response Cell and Forensic Monitoring Task Force

AP's Information & PR Minister Kolusu Parthasarathy said the state will form a "social media response cell" and a task force to investigate "unethical practices on social media" — including harassment of women, disinformation about the government, and smear campaigns against politicians — using advanced forensic tools backed by a dedicated budget. The move follows a string of arrests of YouTubers and comedians in the state and geo-blocking of opposition and activist content, raising censorship concerns. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- MediaNama

Allahabad HC Seeks Centre's Reply on PIL to Bar Minors From Roblox and Similar Gaming Platforms

The Lucknow Bench issued notices to the Union government, MeitY, NCPCR and the new Online Gaming Authority of India on a PIL alleging Roblox and similar platforms (PUBG, Free Fire MAX, GTA, Fortnite) expose minors to grooming, violent content and financial exploitation without effective age verification. The petition arrives just months after India's Online Gaming Rules, 2026 took effect on May 1. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- MediaNama

Notable Tech Industry Events

NABARD and NSDC Launch 'Gramodyam' Rural Entrepreneurship Programme on NABARD's 45th Foundation Day

Launched by NABARD Chairman Dr. Shaji K V at the institution's 45th Foundation Day event, Gramodyam will be implemented by NSDC with the Institute for Industrial Development to identify, train and support rural entrepreneurs via a hybrid digital/on-ground model — targeting ~4,000 rural entrepreneurs in the three-year pilot, with credit facilitation, mentoring and digital tracking of enterprise creation, UDYAM and GST registrations on NSDC's platform. (Published: 2026-07-13)
- Entrackr

Key Takeaways

  1. India's sovereign-AI stance is hardening into procurement policy. The MeitY memorandum pausing OpenAI/Anthropic cybersecurity model deployments across ministries — even as the Finance Ministry actively sought GPT-5.5 for security work — shows the IndiaAI Mission's indigenous-model agenda now directly gates foreign frontier-model adoption in government.

  2. India's IT-services giants are repositioning around AI deployment rather than resisting it. TCS's plan for up to 8,900 forward-deployed AI engineers plus openness to AI/cybersecurity acquisitions signals the sector's bet that hands-on enterprise AI implementation is the next growth engine.

  3. Southeast Asia's mobile banking-malware threat is regionalising. RedHook's confirmed expansion from Vietnam into Indonesia — using wireless-ADB privilege abuse and reputable cloud hosting for delivery — indicates SEA banking trojans are becoming multi-country campaigns rather than single-market operations.

  4. A heavy day for Indian tech-policy enforcement on multiple fronts at once. TRAI moved against call-tagging apps, Andhra Pradesh announced state-level social media monitoring, and the Allahabad HC opened proceedings on child access to gaming platforms — regulation of India's internet is increasingly driven by regulators, states and courts simultaneously.

  5. Funding news was notably quiet. No major India/SEA venture rounds were confirmed as announced on July 13; the day skewed toward talent moves, product launches and government-backed entrepreneurship infrastructure.
