Daily Deeptech Digest - September 1, 2026

AI, Cybersecurity & Deeptech News from India & Southeast Asia

Coverage Date: Sunday, 31 August 2026

Daily digest of AI, cybersecurity and deeptech developments across India and Southeast Asia. All items below were published or reported on 31 August 2026.

AI & Machine Learning

India's AI Adoption Entering a New Phase, Says India-Singapore Dialogue Co-Chair

AI adoption in India is scaling rapidly beyond experimentation into workflows, productivity and operational efficiency, according to Rajan Navani, Indian co-chair of the 17th India-Singapore Strategic Dialogue, in remarks reported on 31 August. He cited the NASSCOM AI Adoption Index showing 87% of Indian enterprises actively using AI as of December 2025, and flagged that AI-driven business-model disruption remains nascent. The report also highlights Singapore's National AI Impact Programme, which aims to support 10,000 enterprises over three years, backed by over S$1 billion committed to AI R&D through 2030. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- Fiinews

Cybersecurity

Aurora Ransomware Operators Caught Using Cursor AI Agent in Attacks

Threat actors linked to Aurora (Aur0ra) ransomware were observed using the AI-powered coding assistant Cursor to help break into target networks, per findings from India-founded CloudSEK and Gambit Security. An exposed open directory revealed months of AI-assisted attack planning against more than 20 organizations across nine countries between April and July 2026, with domain-level or interactive access gained at 17 victims. Both Windows and Linux versions of Aurora, written in Zig, were identified. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- The Hacker News
- CloudSEK

China-Linked "Fire Ant" Expands Espionage Campaign to Cisco Routers

The China-nexus espionage actor Fire Ant, which overlaps with UNC3886, has expanded beyond VMware hypervisors to compromise Cisco IOS XR routers, TACACS servers, and Linux management hosts — turning routers into traffic-collection platforms while suppressing logging and telemetry. The group explored paths into connected high-value environments including critical infrastructure networks, a pattern highly relevant to telecom and infrastructure operators across Asia. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- The Hacker News
- Cybersecurity Dive

India's Cyber Incident Load and Breach Costs Keep Climbing

Coverage published 31 August of the India-Singapore dialogue surfaced fresh figures on India's threat landscape: CERT-In tracked 29.44 lakh cybersecurity incidents in 2025, up from 20.41 lakh in 2024, while IBM's 2026 Cost of a Data Breach report puts the average Indian breach cost at Rs.25.5 crore, up 15.9% year-on-year. Notably, 26% of malicious breaches in India were AI-generated, while only 32% of organisations report extensive use of AI and security automation. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- Fiinews

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

YOGa Clean Air Raises INR 20 Crore Series A Led by Info Edge Ventures

Gurugram-based indoor air-quality company YOGa Clean Air raised INR 20 crore in its first external round, led by Info Edge Ventures with participation from Zomato/Eternal founder Deepinder Goyal and Three Words Capital. The deeptech firm's patented Clean Air Bubble technology filters incoming air and manages CO2 levels; funds will drive expansion into new Tier-1/Tier-2 cities and public-transport applications. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- StartupTalky

Groww AMC Closes INR 580 Crore Strategic Investment from State Street

Groww Asset Management completed a strategic investment of up to INR 580 crore from State Street Investment Management, closing a deal first announced in January. State Street takes a 23% economic interest and 4.85% voting rights, giving the fintech-led AMC access to State Street's indexing, ETF and systematic-investing expertise as its AUM nears INR 4,730 crore. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- StartupTalky

Truemeds in Talks to Acquire DealShare at ~95% Below Peak Valuation

E-pharmacy Truemeds is reportedly in talks to acquire social-commerce unicorn DealShare for about $90 million in an all-stock deal — roughly 95% below DealShare's 2022 unicorn-peak valuation, in another marker of the correction sweeping India's consumer-tech sector. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- StartupTalky

E-Grocery Startup Satvacart Shuts Down After 12 Years

Gurugram-based Satvacart ceased operations with 28 August as its last working day, founder Rahul H. Saxena announced. One of the early online grocery players to turn profitable (2019), it could not sustain scale against quick-commerce giants Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart — a signal of continuing consolidation pressure on smaller e-commerce players. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- StartupTalky

Regional Tech Policy & Regulations

NPCI to Make UPI AutoPay Mandates Portable Across Apps

The National Payments Corporation of India is reportedly set to make UPI AutoPay interoperable, letting users shift existing mandates between UPI apps and letting merchants switch payment gateways without customer re-registration. The features are expected to be announced at the Global Fintech Fest in Mumbai in September, as top-10 banks processed nearly 1.8 billion e-mandate transactions in July 2026 — more than triple the year-ago volume. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- StartupTalky

Notable Tech Industry Events

17th India-Singapore Strategic Dialogue: AI as a New Layer of the Bilateral Relationship

Detailed coverage published 31 August of the 17th India-Singapore Strategic Dialogue (held in Singapore, organised by ISAS-NUS with Ananta Centre and CII) outlined how the two countries plan to combine complementary strengths — Singapore's institutional capability, capital and ASEAN connectivity with India's scale, talent and digital public infrastructure — across AI, cybersecurity, skills and enterprise adoption. Cyber-collaboration was flagged as an urgent joint priority. (Published: 2026-08-31)
- Fiinews

Key Takeaways

  • AI is now both weapon and shield: the Aurora/Cursor findings and India's 26% AI-generated breach share show attackers operationalizing AI faster than most enterprises are deploying AI-powered defenses (only 32% report extensive security automation).
  • India-Singapore AI corridor is deepening, with the Strategic Dialogue positioning AI and cybersecurity as the next layer of the bilateral economic relationship.
  • Deeptech funding continues in focused pockets — YOGa Clean Air's Series A shows investor appetite for patented hard-tech (clean air) even as consumer-tech consolidates (DealShare's ~95% valuation haircut, Satvacart's shutdown).
  • Fintech infrastructure keeps compounding: UPI AutoPay portability and the Groww-State Street close both point to India's payments and asset-management rails maturing toward interoperability and institutional depth.
  • Critical-infrastructure espionage is intensifying across Asia, with Fire Ant's expansion into network-layer devices a warning for telecom and government network operators in the region.
