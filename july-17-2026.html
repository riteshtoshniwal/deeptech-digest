AI & Machine Learning

Emergent Becomes India's Second AI Unicorn in a Month

Bengaluru-founded AI startup Emergent raised a $300 million Series C at a $1.5 billion valuation, becoming India's second AI unicorn within a month. The round was led by Creaegis, joined by Claypond and Sentinel Global, with existing investors Khosla Ventures, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Lightspeed, and Y Combinator participating. Emergent's vibe-coding platform targets non-technical founders — roughly 70% of its users have no prior coding experience. (Published: 2026-07-16)

Cybersecurity

"AI Asymmetry" Flagged as Defining Cyber Risk for India's BFSI Sector

The second edition of the Digital Threat Report 2025–26 — jointly produced by MeitY, CERT-In, CSIRT-Fin and SISA — dominated India cybersecurity coverage on July 16. It warns of "AI Asymmetry": attackers operating at machine speed while defenders remain bound by human-paced procedures, and notes six of seven predictions from last year's edition have fully materialised. (Report released earlier in the week; featured prominently in July 16 coverage and current-affairs roundups)

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

IFC Plans Up to $175M Backing for AirTrunk's Malaysia Data Centres

The International Finance Corporation is planning up to $175 million in debt-and-equity financing for AirTrunk's data centre campus in Malaysia — another signal of the AI-infrastructure capital wave sweeping Southeast Asia, where data centres took the bulk of H1 2026's $7.4B in regional tech funding. (Published: 2026-07-16)

Ather Energy Approves ₹1,200 Cr Preferential Raise from Hero MotoCorp, India-Japan Fund

EV maker Ather Energy's board approved a ₹1,200 crore preferential fundraise: Hero MotoCorp invests ₹960 crore via convertible warrants, NIIF's India-Japan Fund takes ~₹200 crore in equity, and co-founders Tarun Mehta and Swapnil Jain add ₹20 crore each. Proceeds support manufacturing expansion, R&D and new products. (Board approval 2026-07-15; widely reported 2026-07-15/16)

Regional Tech Policy & Regulations

Union Cabinet Clears India Semiconductor Mission 2.0 with ₹1.27 Lakh Crore Outlay

India's Cabinet approved ISM 2.0 with a ₹1.27 lakh crore (~$15B) outlay and an extended 12-year programme lifecycle to reassure long-horizon fab investors. The second phase shifts focus from capacity-building to vertical integration, with the government expecting ~₹4 lakh crore in investments and ₹2 lakh crore in semiconductor production over the scheme period. (Approved 2026-07-15; front-page coverage 2026-07-16)
- Business Standard
- Saachi Baat — Top News 16 July 2026

Notable Tech & Regional Events

India Hosts 5th BIMSTEC National Security Advisers' Meeting in New Delhi

India hosted the 5th BIMSTEC NSA-level meeting on July 16, bringing together South and Southeast Asian members on regional security cooperation — including cybersecurity, maritime security and transnational-crime coordination across the Bay of Bengal region. (Held: 2026-07-16)
- Legacy IAS — Current Affairs 16 July 2026

Key Takeaways

  1. India's AI moment is compounding — a second AI unicorn (Emergent) inside a month, on the heels of Semiconductor Mission 2.0, shows capital and policy converging on India's AI stack, even as the country still lacks a frontier foundation model and domestic cutting-edge chips.
  2. Infrastructure is eating SEA's funding pie — IFC's AirTrunk Malaysia backing continues the pattern that defined H1 2026, where data centres captured most of the region's $7.4B in tech funding.
  3. AI-vs-AI is now the central cyber narrative — the MeitY/CERT-In/SISA report's "AI Asymmetry" framing puts machine-speed offense against human-speed defense at the top of the BFSI risk agenda.
  4. Strategic autonomy is the policy throughline — ISM 2.0's 12-year horizon and the BIMSTEC security meeting both reflect India positioning for long-term technological and regional resilience.
