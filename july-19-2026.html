Deep Tech & SpaceTech

Skyroot's Vikram-1 Reaches Orbit — India's First Private Orbital Launch

Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1 rocket aced its debut flight on Saturday morning, lifting off from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre after a 35-minute technical hold and placing its upper stage into the planned 450-km low Earth orbit roughly 15 minutes after liftoff. The milestone makes Skyroot the first private Indian company to reach orbit — and on its first attempt — putting India among the handful of nations with private orbital launch capability. The rocket carried Skyroot's SCOPE data-collection satellite alongside domestic, international and symbolic payloads. (Published: 2026-07-18)

AI & Machine Learning

Indonesia Joins China-Led World AI Cooperation Organization as Founding Member

At a ceremony in Shanghai, Indonesia joined 28 other economies — including China, Brazil and Russia — in establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), a Shanghai-headquartered body that aims to encourage international cooperation, set ethical standards and ensure AI develops safely and inclusively. The move highlights Indonesia's growing strategic weight in Beijing's push to build a China-led AI ecosystem across the Global South, with Southeast Asia's largest economy becoming a key arena in the global AI competition. (Published: 2026-07-18)
- The China-Global South Project

Cybersecurity

GoSerpent Backdoor Looted Government and Biometric Data Across Southeast Asia

Kaspersky researchers disclosed GoSerpent, a previously undocumented backdoor used in a long-running espionage campaign against government and diplomatic entities across Southeast Asia, harvesting police complaint-management systems, biometric databases, criminal case files and records linked to national identity registries. The malware staged stolen data locally and harvested credentials before quietly exfiltrating archives over network shares, and Kaspersky assesses a probable (unconfirmed) link to the TetrisPhantom threat actor. Coverage and analysis of the campaign continued through July 18. (Reported: 2026-07-17; continued coverage 2026-07-18)

Inc42's weekly funding roundup, published Saturday, reported that Indian startups cumulatively raised about $281.4 Mn across 24 deals between July 11 and 17 — more than 3X the previous week's $71.9 Mn. The surge was driven by AI startup Emergent's $130 Mn round at a valuation beyond $1.5 Bn, minting India's newest unicorn, alongside wealthtech firm Neo Group's ₹350 Cr raise led by Peak XV Partners. Key Takeaways

India's private space sector crossed a defining threshold with Skyroot's Vikram-1 reaching orbit on its first attempt — a validation of the country's post-2020 space liberalisation and a signal to global smallsat customers that a second Indian launch provider is now operational. On the threat landscape, the GoSerpent disclosures underline how patient, staged-exfiltration espionage campaigns have been quietly mining Southeast Asian government identity and biometric systems for years — a reminder that regional public-sector networks remain prime targets. And India's venture funding showed renewed momentum, with weekly inflows tripling on the back of Emergent's unicorn round, reinforcing AI as the dominant theme in Indian dealmaking.
