AI & Machine Learning

AI-Driven Job Uncertainty Among Indian Youth Flagged by Former NITI Aayog Vice Chairman

In a PTI interview published August 2, former NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Rajiv Kumar said the rise of AI has created uncertainty and frustration among Indian youth about employability and job creation, citing a roughly 20% unemployment rate in the 15-29 age group per the latest PLFS data. He called for sustained dialogue between policymakers and young people, and suggested the Nandan Nilekani-led examination reform task force engage directly with youth. He also urged India to target double-digit growth and state-specific export policies to become an upper-middle-income country by 2047. (Published: 2026-08-02)

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

India Startup Funding Fell 14.3% QoQ to $4.08B in Q2 2026 — DealStreetAsia Weekly Wrap

DealStreetAsia's Editor's Take, published August 2, spotlighted its India Deal Review: Q2 2026, which found PE/VC investment in Indian startups declined 14.3% to $4.08 billion across 261 deals in April-June, from $4.76 billion in Q1. Deal volume fell more sharply (-25.4% from 350 deals) amid tighter scrutiny of early-stage bets, while about half the quarter's capital came from seven megadeals, signaling renewed late-stage appetite. (Published: 2026-08-02)

Key Takeaways

India's funding reset continues. The Q2 2026 numbers (-14.3% QoQ in value, -25.4% in volume) confirm investors are concentrating capital in fewer, larger late-stage deals while tightening early-stage scrutiny — a barbell that favours proven growth-stage companies and disciplined seed funds like Anicut's new vehicle.

AI is now a labour-politics issue in India. A former NITI Aayog vice chairman publicly linking AI to youth unemployment anxiety — weeks after protests toppled the education minister — signals that AI's employment impact is moving to the centre of Indian policy debate.

SEA's platform economy is proving profitability. GoTo, Atome, and VNG all posting strong profitability metrics in the same week suggests the region's tech majors have durably shifted from growth-at-all-costs to sustainable economics, even as regulatory risks (like Indonesia's commission cap) persist.

AI infrastructure capital keeps flowing to SEA. AirTrunk's $2.3B Malaysia green loan and rising sovereign-fund participation underline that the region's data centre build-out remains one of Asia's biggest deeptech capital stories.
