AI & Machine Learning

Global AI Alliance launches its first joint research initiative

The Global AI Alliance marked the official launch of its inaugural joint research initiative, aimed at standardising AI safety and technical research across borders. The move is positioned as a step toward aligning international AI standards after years of fragmented, country-by-country policy discussion, with the alliance seeking universal safety benchmarks and collaborative technical research. (Published: 2026-06-08)

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

Immuneel Therapeutics raises over Rs 100 Cr in Series B

Bengaluru-based cancer biotech Immuneel Therapeutics, co-founded by Biocon's Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, closed a Series B of over Rs 100 crore (~$10.5 million). The round drew Singularity AMC, Zerodha's Rainmatter and several HNIs alongside existing backers Eight Roads Ventures and F-Prime Capital. Proceeds will expand GMP manufacturing, accelerate commercialisation of Qartemi — described as India's first approved CAR-T therapy for non-Hodgkin lymphoma and leukaemia — and fund expansion across Asia-Pacific and the Middle East. (Published: 2026-06-08)
- Entrackr
- DealStreetAsia
- Deccan Herald

Curefoods puts IPO plans on hold amid market volatility

Cloud-kitchen company Curefoods deferred its planned IPO, joining PhonePe and Flipkart in pausing public listings amid choppy markets. Reports indicate mutual funds resisted the roughly Rs 4,000 crore valuation Curefoods was seeking for its ~Rs 800 crore issue; the company is expected to revisit a listing next year if conditions improve. (Published: 2026-06-08)
- Inc42
- Outlook Money

Cybersecurity

Watch item: actively-exploited SolarWinds Serv-U flaw still driving response

The most prominent threat in the current news cycle is CVE-2026-28318, a high-severity denial-of-service flaw in SolarWinds Serv-U that CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after evidence of active exploitation. Remote, unauthenticated attackers can crash the service via crafted HTTP POST requests using a Content-Encoding: deflate header; SolarWinds has shipped a fix in 15.5.4 HF1, and US federal civilian agencies must remediate by June 19, 2026. (KEV addition: 2026-06-05; remained a lead story through June 8)

Key Takeaways

  • Biotech deeptech keeps drawing capital. Immuneel's Rs 100 Cr Series B — into India's first approved CAR-T therapy — underlines that patient, science-heavy deeptech is still funding even in a selective market.
  • The IPO window is narrowing. Curefoods joining PhonePe and Flipkart in pausing listings signals continued public-market caution toward loss-making consumer-tech names; valuation expectations are the sticking point.
  • AI governance is consolidating globally. The Global AI Alliance's joint research launch reflects a shift from fragmented national AI policy toward shared safety standards — relevant to how India and SEA will plug into international frameworks.
  • Patch discipline matters. The actively-exploited SolarWinds Serv-U DoS flaw is a reminder that file-transfer infrastructure remains a favoured target; the June 19 federal remediation deadline is a useful benchmark for private operators too.
