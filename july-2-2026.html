Fintech, Regulation & Tech Policy

Dream Sports shuts down wealthtech platform Dream Money

Dream Sports (parent of Dream11) confirmed it is winding down its fintech platform Dream Money barely a year after its August 2025 launch, with the app remaining accessible until July 30 before operations cease. New registrations, fresh investments and loan applications have stopped; existing mutual-fund folios, fixed deposits and loans continue via partner institutions, and digital-gold holdings migrate to Augmont. The shutdown follows India's 2025 ban on real-money gaming (including fantasy sports) and the Supreme Court's ruling upholding a 28% GST on the full face value of bets — regulatory shocks that derailed Dream Sports' diversification push. (Published: 2026-07-01)

Data protection compliance clock tightens for large fiduciaries

India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework continues its phased 2026-27 rollout, and MeitY is consulting industry on cutting the compliance window for significant data fiduciaries from 18 months to 12. Penalties run up to ₹250 crore for failure to implement reasonable security safeguards. Surveys still show ~70% of organisations report limited familiarity with the Act — keeping DPDP readiness a live boardroom issue into H2 2026. (Context as of 2026-07-01; framework notified 2025-11-13)
