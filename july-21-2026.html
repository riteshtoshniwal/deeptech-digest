AI & Machine Learning

OpenAI Elevates Indian-Origin Engineer Uday Ruddarraju to CTO for Compute

OpenAI promoted Uday Ruddarraju to Chief Technology Officer for Compute, a move widely covered by Indian tech media given his Indian roots. The appointment underscores how access to large-scale computing infrastructure — processing power, networking, and data centers — has become decisive in the race to train and deploy advanced foundation models. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- YourStory

KPMG and Singapore's National Library Board Launch AI-Misinformation Resilience Drive

KPMG in Singapore and the National Library Board launched "Read to Lead: Building an AI-Ready Mind in Singapore," a year-long programme backed by the Ministry of Digital Development and Information. It follows a poll of 1,150 professionals in which only four in ten were confident they could distinguish accurate content from AI-generated misinformation; the partners will co-develop a practical toolkit covering AI literacy, misinformation, and cyber risks. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- SecurityBrief Asia

Cybersecurity

Everest Ransomware Gang Lists Indian IT Firm Alzone Software, Claims 145GB of Data

Alzone Software, an Indian IT company specialising in robotic process automation, AI, cloud, and enterprise software, was listed as a victim by the Everest ransomware group. The dark-web listing, discovered on July 20, 2026, claims a leak of 145GB of company data, adding to a string of extortion attacks on Indian technology service providers this year. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- Breachsense

LockBit Claims Breach of Singapore ICT and Cybersecurity Provider Adventus

Adventus, a Singapore-based provider of ICT infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud services, and managed IT support, was posted to the LockBit ransomware leak site on July 20, 2026, with the size of the leak still unknown. The attack is notable because the victim is itself a managed IT and security services provider, raising potential downstream supply-chain risk for its regional clients. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- Breachsense

Manufacturers Strengthen OT Security as Threats Intensify, Says Rockwell Automation

SecurityBrief India published an interview with Inba Rathinam, VP and Head of Regions for Life Cycle Services at Rockwell Automation, on how manufacturers across energy, mining, food, and pharma are investing in dedicated operational technology (OT) security. Rathinam warned that air-gapping is no longer sufficient as industrial systems become connected, and flagged an emerging AI "arms race" between industrial defenders and threat actors. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- SecurityBrief India

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

Raghu Vamsi Aerospace Raises $40M to Scale Defence Deep Tech and Autonomous Systems

Hyderabad-based Raghu Vamsi Aerospace Group announced a $40 million (~Rs 400 crore) Series B round led by Norwest and Skegen Asset Management, with participation from IndusBridge Ventures, GJNX Ventures, and Ashish Kacholia. The capital will expand manufacturing across India, the UK, and the US, and deepen its Mission Systems and Deep-Tech & Autonomous Systems verticals, including its ARROBOT platform for drones and unmanned ground vehicles for India's armed forces. (Published: 2026-07-20)

- The AI Journal

Wealthtech Firm Veriqus Raises Rs 387 Cr (~$40M) Led by Norwest

Mumbai-based Veriqus Group, founded by Roshi Jain and Anand Khatau, raised Rs 387 crore in a round led by Norwest Venture Partners to expand its network of wealth creators and strengthen its AI-enabled wealth management platform. The company works with over 500 wealth creators managing assets worth more than Rs 1.2 lakh crore and plans to deepen its presence in Tier II and other high-growth Indian cities. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- Entrackr

Quick Medicine Delivery Startup Plazza Raises $15M Series A Co-Led by Accel, Elevation, Nexus

Bengaluru-based Plazza raised $15 million in a Series A co-led by Accel, Elevation Capital, and Nexus Venture Partners, with participation from All In Capital and Better Capital. The pharmacy-first quick-commerce startup delivers medicines in 15-30 minutes and will use the funds to strengthen its tech platform, deepen AI-driven inventory intelligence, and expand its pharmacy network; GMV grew nearly 27x between June 2025 and March 2026. (Published: 2026-07-20)

Regional Tech Policy & Regulations

SEBI Warns Regulated Entities Against AI-Powered "Boss Scam" Impersonation Fraud

India's markets regulator SEBI cautioned regulated entities and listed companies against a growing cyber fraud in which attackers impersonate CEOs and senior officials using AI-generated deepfakes, voice cloning, and malware to trick finance teams into transferring funds to mule accounts. The alert, flagged by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), advises firms to independently verify payment instructions and avoid acting on digital instructions alone. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- MediaNama

Internet Shutdown Imposed Around Delhi's Jantar Mantar During Protest March

Mobile internet was suspended in parts of central Delhi as crowds gathered at Jantar Mantar following the forced removal of activist Sonam Wangchuk, with reports of signal jammers deployed at the site. The Internet Freedom Foundation said no suspension order was made public and that the shutdown breached Supreme Court safeguards under Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India and the Telecommunications (Temporary Suspension of Services) Rules, 2024. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- MediaNama

Notable Tech Industry Events

The ePlane Company Unveils Full-Scale Prototype of Its e200X eVTOL Aircraft

Chennai-based deep-tech aerospace startup The ePlane Company, incubated at IIT Madras, unveiled the first full-scale prototype of its electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft, the e200X (PT-01), at its new 60,000 sq ft production facility in Chennai. The all-carbon-fibre aircraft weighs up to 2.2 tonnes, is designed to operate from existing helipads and open spaces, and is slated for certified flight testing by mid-2027. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- YourStory

SG Finserve, BharatPe Money and Succesship Launch Digital Lending Solution for MSME Merchants

NBFC SG Finserve partnered with BharatPe Money and fintech infrastructure firm Succesship Technologies to launch a fully digital, paperless lending solution for India's micro, small, and medium enterprises. Built in line with the RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines, the offering emphasizes customer consent, transparency, and regulatory compliance, with simplified applications and faster approvals for eligible merchants. (Published: 2026-07-20)
- YourStory

Key Takeaways

  • Defence and aerospace deep tech is drawing serious capital: Raghu Vamsi's $40M round and ePlane's e200X prototype unveiling on the same day signal accelerating investor and industrial momentum behind India's dual-use and advanced manufacturing ecosystem.
  • Norwest had a double-header day in India, leading both Raghu Vamsi's $40M defence-tech round and Veriqus's Rs 387 Cr wealthtech round — a sign of large crossover funds deploying aggressively across Indian sectors.
  • IT service providers are becoming prime ransomware targets: Everest's listing of India's Alzone Software and LockBit's claim against Singapore's Adventus — itself a security services provider — highlight growing supply-chain risk across the region's IT services sector.
  • Regulators are catching up with AI-enabled fraud: SEBI's "Boss Scam" advisory and Singapore's KPMG-NLB AI-literacy programme show both countries moving from awareness to structured countermeasures against deepfakes and AI misinformation.
