AI and Machine Learning

ekincare Acquires AI Claims Platform Superclaims

Digital health platform ekincare announced the 100% acquisition of Superclaims, an AI-powered claims adjudication SaaS platform that cuts health insurance claim processing from roughly 90 minutes to under 5 minutes. The deal brings insurers and TPAs onto ekincare's platform alongside employers, employees and providers, positioning the combined entity to expand globally as an AI-powered insurance SaaS. (Published: 2026-07-06)

Indian Stocks Get a Fresh Look as Global Investors Cool on the AI Trade

Bloomberg's markets newsletter reported that as the global AI-trade frenzy cools, foreign investors are reconsidering Indian equities as a defensive hedge against an "AI storm." TCS was set to kick off earnings season with its AI commentary closely watched. (Published: 2026-07-06)

Cybersecurity

Capillary Technologies Loses Over Rs 32 Cr in Deepfake-Enabled Cyber Fraud

Bengaluru-based listed SaaS firm Capillary Technologies disclosed in a stock exchange filing on July 6 that a recently acquired overseas subsidiary was hit by a cyber-enabled banking fraud of ~EUR 3 million (Rs 32.7 crore), executed using deepfake voice cloning, forged signatures and social engineering to impersonate key managerial personnel. The company has recovered EUR 0.45 million so far, frozen additional traced funds, and says no customer or employee data was compromised. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- Entrackr
- Inc42

Deep Tech Startups and Investments

Suzuki's Next Bharat Ventures Launches Rs 2,000 Cr Second Fund for Indian Impact Startups

Next Bharat Ventures announced its second impact fund with a Rs 2,000 crore corpus — among India's largest impact-focused VC funds — with Suzuki Motor Corporation as anchor investor and additional capital from other Japanese corporations. The fund targets early and growth-stage startups in rural healthcare, mobility, fintech, agritech, cleantech and retail tech, with ticket sizes of $500K–$1 Mn; half the corpus goes to a fund-of-funds strategy. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- Inc42
- Business News This Week

SEA Tech Funding Doubles to $7.4B in H1 2026, Driven by Mega-Rounds

Southeast Asian tech companies raised a combined $7.4 billion in H1 2026 — more than double the $3.2 billion a year earlier — even as round counts and first-time funded companies declined. Enterprise infrastructure, enterprise applications and fintech led, anchored by DayOne's $4.5B Series C for data centers; the half saw 12 rounds of $100M+, and the largest exit was KKR–Singtel's $5.2B acquisition of ST Telemedia Global Data Centers. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- TechNode Global

Samsung to Add $1B to Its $24B Vietnam Investment in 2026

Samsung Electronics plans to invest a further $1 billion in Vietnam in 2026, on top of roughly $24 billion in cumulative investment through end-2025, per CFO Park Soon Cheol — with medium and long-term plans focused on electronics, display and electromechanical sectors. Samsung runs six plants and an R&D center in Vietnam generating over $60B in annual revenue. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- TechNode Global

Notable Tech Industry Events

Info Edge Takes Full Control of Coding Ninjas

Info Edge's board approved (at its July 6 meeting) acquiring the remaining 45.36% stake in Coding Ninjas from its founders for ~Rs 39.91 crore, taking ownership from 51% to 100%. The Naukri parent said the deal will deepen Naukri–Coding Ninjas synergies, including building "mini AI course" offerings; the board also approved an additional Rs 180 Cr commitment to B8 Fund I. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- Entrackr

Nikhil Kamath's WTF Acquires Creative Agency BTG

WTF, led by Zerodha co-founder Nikhil Kamath, acquired Mumbai-based creative agency BTG (By The Gram) — its second creative-services acquisition after One Hand Clap — as it builds a portfolio of founder-led creative businesses across branding, content and strategy. BTG, whose clients include Netflix, IKEA, Volkswagen and Bumble, will operate independently under its existing leadership. (Published: 2026-07-06)
- Entrackr

YourStory Daily Roundup: Oben Electric Deliveries, MetaGO Launch

YourStory's July 6 roundup covered Oben Electric starting customer deliveries of its Rorr Evo e-motorcycle after 25,000+ bookings in 15 days, actor-entrepreneur Kunal Kapoor launching doctor-led metabolic health platform MetaGO, and Simple Energy partnering with IDFC FIRST Bank for EV scooter financing. (Published: 2026-07-06)

Key Takeaways

  1. Deepfake-enabled financial fraud has hit corporate India at scale — Capillary Technologies' EUR 3M loss via voice cloning and executive impersonation is one of the most concrete examples yet of AI-powered attacks on listed Indian tech firms, and dominated July 6 cybersecurity coverage.
  2. SEA funding is recovering on concentration, not breadth — H1 2026 funding doubled to $7.4B, but with fewer rounds and fewer first-time funded companies; a handful of mega-deals is doing the heavy lifting.
  3. Japanese and Korean capital is deepening regional bets — Suzuki anchoring a Rs 2,000 Cr India impact fund and Samsung committing another $1B to Vietnam on the same day underline East Asian corporates' sustained investment push.
  4. Consolidation is a theme in Indian tech — Info Edge buying out Coding Ninjas (with an explicit AI-courses rationale), ekincare acquiring Superclaims, and WTF's second agency acquisition all landed on July 6.
  5. Policy lull after a busy July 1 — no fresh India/SEA tech policy news broke on July 6; the notable regulatory moves (Vietnam cybersecurity law, Indonesia ride-hailing cap) took effect earlier in the week.
