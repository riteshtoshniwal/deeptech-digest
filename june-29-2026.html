AI & Machine Learning

Digital India turns 11: government re-centres strategy on AI and semiconductors

As the Digital India programme approached its 11th anniversary (July 1), the government detailed a strategic pivot toward artificial intelligence and semiconductor manufacturing. It has approved investments worth ~₹1.64 lakh crore across 12 semiconductor projects and is building a national AI ecosystem backed by 45,000+ GPUs under the IndiaAI Mission (outlay ₹10,372 crore+). AI Kosh now hosts 12,500+ datasets, 307 AI models and 20 toolkits, with 15 large and small language models supported under the AI Foundation Model pillar. (Published: 2026-06-28; initial coverage 2026-06-27)

IndiaAI Startups Global: application window for 2nd cohort closes

June 28, 2026 marked the application deadline for IndiaAI Startups Global, the international acceleration programme for Indian AI startups run in collaboration with Station F and HEC Paris (Paris). The programme is one of the talent and globalisation pillars of the IndiaAI Mission, helping selected startups access European markets, investors and infrastructure. (Deadline: 2026-06-28)

Cybersecurity

CERT-In and enterprise spend pivot as AI-driven threats mount in India

Indian enterprise cybersecurity spending is being reshaped by AI-enabled threats, with cybersecurity emerging as a core pillar of enterprise AI adoption. CERT-In has warned that advances in frontier AI systems could materially enhance attacker capabilities, prompting organisations to prioritise AI-security governance. Analysts note agentic AI could open $300–400 billion in addressable technology-services spend by 2030, spanning cybersecurity, legacy modernisation and governance. (Reported: 2026-06-26)
- Business Standard

ClickFix malware campaigns expand with new loaders and fake-update lures

Threat researchers reported continued expansion of "ClickFix" social-engineering campaigns, which trick users into running malicious commands via fake update and CAPTCHA-style prompts, now deploying new loaders. India remains among the most-targeted countries in concurrent phishing operations, with 33,000+ unique users hit globally since April 2026. (Reported: late June 2026)
- The Hacker News

Regional Tech (Southeast Asia)

Vietnam emerges as Southeast Asia's AI data-centre hotspot

Vietnam has drawn more than $7 billion in announced and proposed AI and data-centre investment in recent months, including a $2B G42-led "AI Factory" in Ho Chi Minh City and ST Telemedia–VNG facilities. Regionally, Singapore still captures ~99% of SEA's disclosed AI-infrastructure funding ($1.2B, 2019–2026), while Malaysia (Johor) and Indonesia (Batam) build sovereign AI corridors.
