🔒 Cybersecurity

India Government Strengthens Cyber Checks Amid AI-Driven Threats

India's government has significantly intensified its cybersecurity monitoring and inspection systems following growing concerns over AI-driven cyber risks. The Ministry of Electronics and IT, alongside CERT-In, is enforcing stricter guidelines as emerging frontier AI systems become capable of automating cyberattacks, identifying vulnerabilities, and executing intrusion operations at unprecedented speed. India is now strengthening real-time network monitoring and encouraging adoption of AI-based defensive tools across critical infrastructure. (Published: 2026-05-31)
- Edunovations Current Affairs

PAN-OS GlobalProtect Authentication Bypass CVE-2026-0257 Now Actively Exploited

Palo Alto Networks confirmed on May 30 that CVE-2026-0257, a medium-severity authentication bypass in PAN-OS and Prisma Access GlobalProtect, is being actively exploited in the wild. The flaw allows attackers to bypass security restrictions and establish unauthorized VPN connections on firewalls with GlobalProtect portal or gateway configured with authentication override cookies enabled. The company had first disclosed the issue on May 13, 2026, and issued updated guidance with patches now available. (Published: 2026-05-30)
- The Hacker News

ChatGPhish: ChatGPT Summaries Weaponized for Phishing Attacks

Permiso Security disclosed a novel attack technique called ChatGPhish, exploiting ChatGPT's implicit trust in Markdown links and images. An attacker appends a small payload to any webpage the victim prompts ChatGPT to summarize, causing ChatGPT to auto-fetch attacker-hosted images that leak victim IP addresses, User-Agent strings, and Referer details. The attack also renders live, clickable phishing links inside the trusted ChatGPT UI — raising critical concerns for enterprise users across India and Southeast Asia relying on AI assistants. (Published: 2026-05-29)
- The Hacker News

LLM Agents Used for Post-Exploitation After Marimo CVE-2026-39987

Security researchers at Sysdig documented a real-world attack where a threat actor used an LLM agent for post-exploitation after compromising a publicly accessible Marimo notebook via CVE-2026-39987 (pre-auth RCE). The AI-assisted attacker extracted cloud credentials, retrieved SSH keys from AWS Secrets Manager, and exfiltrated an entire PostgreSQL database in under two minutes — marking one of the first confirmed in-the-wild uses of autonomous LLM agents for cyberattack post-exploitation. (Published: 2026-05-29)
- The Hacker News

Kimsuky North Korean Hackers Target South Korea with HTTPSpy, HelloDoor & VS Code Tunnels

The North Korean threat actor Kimsuky expanded its cyber arsenal with new malware targeting South Korean military and corporate entities in March–April 2026. The group deployed HTTPSpy (disguised as security software installers), a Rust-based PebbleDash variant called HelloDoor believed to be developed with LLM assistance, and VS Code tunneling mechanisms for persistence. The campaign leveraged fake Webex meeting pages and spoofed Korean B2B security software — a direct threat to South Korean and regional tech targets across Asia. (Published: 2026-05-29)
- The Hacker News
- SC Media

A new analysis published May 30 documents how a new breed of AI-native companies is emerging across Asia — distinct from traditional tech giants in that AI is built into their core product architecture from day one. Companies across India, Singapore, and Japan are leading this shift, with Southeast Asia increasingly positioned as a talent pool and deployment testing ground for AI-native applications in fintech, logistics, and healthcare. Agentic AI is gaining particular momentum, with 42% of APAC enterprises planning to spend at least $1 million on AI agents over the next 12 months (Omdia, May 2026). India's $931M DeepTech Funding Surge Signals Structural Shift

India's DeepTech ecosystem recorded a $931 million funding surge in recent months, with AI accounting for 91% of all deeptech investment. The country now hosts over 4,200 DeepTech startups with 550 new companies added in the past year. India is the world's second-largest GenAI startup ecosystem with ~890 GenAI startups. The government offers GPU compute at ₹67/hour for startups and a 100% compute subsidy for AI foundational model builders under the IndiaAI Mission. (Published: 2026-05-26)

🔑 Key Takeaways

  1. AI-powered cyberattacks are accelerating — India's CERT-In is raising alarms as LLM-assisted attacks (demonstrated by Sysdig's Marimo case) move from theoretical to operational. Defenders need AI-speed response capabilities.

  2. The PAN-OS VPN bypass (CVE-2026-0257) is being actively exploited — organizations in India and SE Asia using Palo Alto GlobalProtect should patch immediately or apply virtual patching via IPS/WAF.

  3. India's deeptech funding momentum is structural, not cyclical — ₹800 crore from Piper Serica and $100M from Shastra VC in the same month signal that patient capital is flowing into IP-led, hard-tech sectors like semiconductors, defence, and AI infrastructure.

  4. SE Asia is transitioning from AI experimentation to AI deployment — with 81% of enterprises beyond the pilot stage and billions planned for agentic AI, the region is becoming a genuine AI operating environment, not just a testing ground.

  5. North Korean and Chinese threat actors remain highly active against Asian targets — Kimsuky's AI-assisted malware development and SHADOW-EARTH-053's Exchange exploits across India, Malaysia, Thailand, and Myanmar underscore ongoing state-linked cyber espionage risk.
