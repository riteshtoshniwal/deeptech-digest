AI & Machine Learning

Junkies Coder launches Agentic AI & Enterprise Modernization practice

Ahmedabad-based software engineering firm Junkies Coder announced a dedicated Agentic AI & Enterprise Modernization practice built to help enterprises deploy AI agents reliably by first modernizing the legacy systems, cloud infrastructure and delivery pipelines those agents depend on. The unit is designed to move AI agents from pilot to production by upgrading the architecture, infrastructure and DevOps foundations they run on. (Published: 2026-06-16)

ContraVault AI raises ₹29.3 Cr to scale AI procurement intelligence

Bengaluru-based ContraVault AI, which runs an AI-powered procurement and tender-intelligence platform for construction, energy, power, defence and aerospace clients, raised ₹29.3 crore (~$3.1 million) in a pre-Series A round led by Chiratae Ventures with participation from Titan Capital Winners Fund. The startup serves 40 customers including Voltas, Shapoorji Pallonji and the Adani Group, and claims clients cut tender processing time by 60–70%. Funds will support US expansion, product development and hiring. (Published: 2026-06-16)
- StartupTalky

TrackerSuite.AI raises ₹6 Cr for AI business operating system

TrackerSuite.AI, an AI-powered business operating system that helps SMEs digitise and automate operations, sales, workforce and inventory workflows, raised ₹6 crore (~$0.7 million) in a pre-Series A round led by a UAE-based family office advised by Bestvantage Investments. The company says it serves more than 1,800 businesses across 25 countries spanning India, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. (Published: 2026-06-16)

Deep Tech Startups & Investments

SolarSquare raises ₹510 Cr (~$53 Mn) Series C for residential rooftop solar

Full-stack residential solar brand SolarSquare (founded 2015; Shreya Mishra, Neeraj Jain, Nikhil Nahar) raised ₹510 crore (~$53 million) in a Series C round led by existing backer Lightspeed, with Lowercarbon Capital, Rainmatter and Good Capital participating. The cleantech company has now raised over $100 million to date, claims a ₹1,000 crore annual revenue run rate and has served nearly 50,000 homes. Capital will fund city expansion, technology and hiring as India's rooftop solar market benefits from the PM Surya Ghar scheme. (Published: 2026-06-16)

CREST raises $3.1 Mn pre-seed for AI-native family office

CREST, an AI-native fractional family office and asset management firm co-founded by Girish Singhi and Zuhaib Khan, raised $3.1 million (~₹26 crore) in a pre-seed round led by BEENEXT, Sparrow, Shastra VC, DeVC, Warmup Ventures, Atrium Ventures and 91ventures, with angels including Amit Ranjan, Revant Bhate and Shantanu Deshpande. Speedioo raises ₹10 Cr seed for used two-wheeler platform

The EBITDA- and cash-flow-positive startup has crossed ₹30 crore GMV and sold over 4,000 vehicles in the past year; proceeds will fund an AI-native tech stack and expansion into new cities. Turtlemint sets IPO dates; issue to open June 19

Insurtech firm Turtlemint confirmed its IPO will open for public subscription on June 19 and close June 23, with a price band of ₹144–152, valuing the company at about ₹4,513 crore (~$475 million) at the upper end.
