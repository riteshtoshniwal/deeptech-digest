Cybersecurity Threats & Solutions

"FortiBleed": database of ~30,000 working Fortinet logins surfaces, total compromise tops 86,000 devices

Researchers warned that attackers have built and are circulating a database of working usernames and passwords for tens of thousands of internet-facing Fortinet FortiGate devices. Cybernews reported a set of ~30,791 devices, while SOCRadar and SecurityWeek put the verified total of compromised credentials at 86,644 devices across 194 countries in the campaign now dubbed "FortiBleed." Affected organisations reportedly include banks, hospitals, telecom operators, universities and government agencies — a critical-infrastructure exposure highly relevant to Indian and Southeast Asian enterprises running FortiGate appliances. (Published: 2026-06-22)
