🔐 Cybersecurity

China-Aligned APT Group SHADOW-EARTH-053 Targets Asian Governments with ShadowPad

Trend Micro researchers have disclosed details of a sophisticated espionage campaign attributed to a China-aligned threat cluster tracked as SHADOW-EARTH-053, active since at least December 2024. The group targets government and defense sectors across South, East, and Southeast Asia — with confirmed victims in India, Pakistan, Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, and Taiwan — as well as a NATO member state (Poland). Attackers exploit N-day vulnerabilities in Microsoft Exchange and IIS servers, deploy Godzilla web shells for persistent access, and stage the ShadowPad backdoor via DLL sideloading of legitimate signed executables. The Google Threat Intelligence Group has linked the activity to a cluster known as UNC6595. (Published: 2026-05-15)

📋 Policy & Regulation

India's 2026 IT Amendment Rules: AI Content Labeling Mandate Takes Effect

India's 2026 IT Amendment Rules introduce mandatory AI content labeling requirements for online platforms. Platforms must prominently label AI-generated or synthetically altered content, ensure labels and watermarks cannot be removed or suppressed, and obtain user declarations for AI-modified content. Non-consensual intimate AI-generated imagery must be removed within 2 hours; other AI-generated harmful content must be removed within 3 hours. Global firms face legal risks for non-compliance with these new obligations. (Published: 2026-05-15)
