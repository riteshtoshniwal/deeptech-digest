🤖 AI & Machine Learning

Google Cloud Opens Southeast Asia–Silicon Valley AI Corridor

Google Cloud launched a new cross-border accelerator program connecting 25 Southeast Asian AI startups with technology and investment hubs in California, offering each selected founder up to $350,000 in cloud credits. Announced on Monday, the three-month, equity-free programme targets seed to Series B AI startups from Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam. Partners include Enterprise Singapore, Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi), Vietnam's National Innovation Center (NIC), and SIHUB (Ho Chi Minh City). Participating startups gain early access to Google's latest AI models including Gemini 3.5, plus hands-on training in agentic coding and multi-agent systems. The cohort commences in August 2026. 🛡️ Cybersecurity

Lazarus Group Deploys RemotePE Memory-Only RAT Against Financial & Crypto Firms

Cybersecurity researchers at Fox-IT (NCC Group subsidiary) disclosed a cross-platform malware called RemotePE used by North Korea–linked Lazarus Group against financial and cryptocurrency organizations. The fileless RAT operates entirely in memory, never writing to disk, and employs a three-stage attack chain via DPAPILoader, RemotePELoader, and the core RemotePE trojan. The malware uses ETW patching, Hell's Gate evasion techniques, and DPAPI environmental keying — making it nearly undetectable. Social engineering via fake Telegram personas and fraudulent Calendly domains initiates compromise. Lazarus Group has stolen approximately $577 million in crypto in the first four months of 2026 alone — 76% of all global crypto thefts. (Published: 2026-05-25)

Cybersecurity for SMBs in SEA: Strategic Focus Over Complexity

Kaspersky's General Manager for ASEAN, Simon Tung, outlined a strategic framework for cybersecurity resilience among Southeast Asian SMBs, which account for 94–99.9% of all businesses in the region. Recent Kaspersky data shows SEA businesses faced 18 million+ malicious attacks in 2025, with backdoor attacks surging 17% year-on-year. The op-ed highlights three core challenges — commoditized ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS), phishing attacks exploiting the human element, and the widening cybersecurity skills gap — and recommends consolidating prevention, detection, and response capabilities into manageable platforms for lean teams. (Published: 2026-05-25)

🏗️ Deeptech Infrastructure & Investment

Moody's: South & Southeast Asia Data Centers to Grow at 24% CAGR Through 2030

Moody's Ratings published a major forecast on Monday projecting that South and Southeast Asia's data center market will grow at a CAGR of 24% over the next four to five years, subject to power and permitting constraints. The region currently accounts for over 3.5 GW of operational data center capacity globally, with capacity set to double by 2030. Key growth markets include India, Malaysia (particularly Johor), Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam. Singapore remains the largest hub at ~1.0 GW but faces capacity constraints, prompting a hub-and-spoke expansion model. Chinese operators like GDS Holdings' DayOne and Bridge Data Centres are expanding rapidly across Malaysia, Indonesia, and Thailand — intensifying competition. (Published: 2026-05-25)

HSBC Launches $4B Clean Energy Credit Facility Targeting China-Vietnam Green Trade

HSBC launched a $4 billion Sustainability and Transition Credit Facility for mainland China companies in clean energy and low-carbon sectors, with Vietnam identified as a key destination. The facility covers clean power, transport electrification, data centers, and AI infrastructure. Vietnam is highlighted for its 27.9% renewable energy share of installed capacity and ~40% EV sales penetration (highest in ASEAN). The country's Power Development Plan VIII (PDP VIII) targets over $134 billion in power generation and transmission investment by 2030. The move signals growing China–Vietnam clean tech corridor activity. (Published: 2026-05-25)

🚗 Regional Tech Policy & Industry

Malaysia EV Transition Gathers Pace Amid Structural Hurdles

Malaysia's EV adoption is accelerating, with April 2026 EV registrations reaching ~5,800 units — lifting penetration to 7–8% of total industry volume for the month. Proton's e.MAS series led with 40% market share, while BYD held 24%, followed by Chery (9.5%) and Zeekr (6.1%). The government's evolving fuel subsidy framework (introducing a 200-litre monthly subsidised fuel limit) is reshaping transport cost dynamics. However, analysts caution that infrastructure gaps and affordability constraints will moderate the pace of adoption. Malaysia targets 20% EV share of new vehicle sales by 2030 and 80% (including hybrids) by 2050, but public charging infrastructure is still below 50% of its 2030 target. (Published: 2026-05-25)
