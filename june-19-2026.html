Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

PM Modi outlines India's AI vision at VivaTech 2026, courts global tech players

At the 10th-anniversary edition of the VivaTech summit in Paris, Prime Minister Narendra Modi laid out India's AI roadmap, arguing that democratizing AI is essential so its benefits "reach everyone" — framing AI as "all-inclusive." He pitched India as an investment destination on the back of eased regulation and improved ease of doing business, and showcased the India pavilion's emerging tech, including a 3D-printed rocket engine, advanced genetic-engineering therapies, and AI cancer-detection tools. He cited Amul's voice-first AI assistant "Sarlaben," which helps women dairy farmers manage livestock nutrition and health. (Published: 2026-06-18)

Cybersecurity Threats & Solutions

Access to ~75,000 Fortinet FortiGate devices put up for sale

Security researchers flagged on June 18 that cybercriminals were selling access to roughly 75,000 Fortinet FortiGate firewall devices with VPN and web-management interfaces exposed. The admin credentials on offer appeared legitimate and recently harvested, suggesting a still-live campaign with direct implications for enterprises and government bodies across India and Southeast Asia that run FortiGate at the network edge. (Published: 2026-06-18)

Cybersecurity ranked India's No. 1 national risk for 2026

Context reinforced on June 18 amid the VivaTech investment push: the World Economic Forum's Global Risks Report 2026 ranks cybersecurity as India's top national risk — ahead of economic downturns, climate disasters, and armed conflict. Analysts note attackers, now amplified by AI and automation, are compressing attack timelines from months to hours, with weak API security and underinvestment cited as India's most common breach vectors. Regional Tech Policy & Regulation

India's IT Rules amendment on AI-generated content shapes mid-2026 compliance

The MeitY-notified Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules, 2026, which fold "synthetically generated information" (deepfakes and AI-generated text, image, audio, video) into intermediaries' due-diligence duties, continue to drive platform compliance work through June. IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has also signalled the need for a dedicated AI law addressing deepfakes and AI-driven job displacement. (Background; amendment notified Feb 2026, active compliance ongoing June 2026)

Southeast Asia Tech

Singapore dominates Southeast Asia's AI funding

Per a Google–Temasek–Bain study circulating this week, ~680 AI startups attracted more than US$2.3B across Southeast Asia in the 12 months to June, accounting for 32% of regional private funding. Singapore led overwhelmingly, with 495 AI startups securing US$1.31B — highlighting how concentrated the region's AI capital remains. (Report released week of June 16, 2026)
- Crowdfund Insider
- South China Morning Post

Southeast Asia's AI data-center buildout accelerates

Hyperscale capacity continues expanding across Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines, with the region now hosting 2,000+ data centers and ~$30B in investment projected by 2030 (demand growing ~20% annually through 2028). Singapore remains the connectivity hub while neighbours absorb scale-out capacity. (Ongoing trend, mid-June 2026)
