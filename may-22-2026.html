🤖 AI & Machine Learning

Singapore Closes ATxSummit 2026 with Major AI Partnerships and NVIDIA Research Lab

Singapore's flagship tech gathering ATxSummit 2026 concluded its final day on May 21, drawing over 4,000 leaders from 50+ countries at Capella Singapore. NVIDIA announced the launch of a new AI research lab in Singapore focused on embodied AI and efficient computing — its second research presence in Asia-Pacific. Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI) signed an MOU with OpenAI to launch the "OpenAI for Singapore" initiative, and expanded its National AI Partnership with Google to cover public services, workforce development, and AI governance. Singapore Launches Physical AI Testbed at Punggol Digital District

Singapore's IMDA, JTC, and Singapore Institute of Technology will collaborate with eight companies — including Grab, DHL, Certis, and QuikBot — to trial "physical AI" robotics in a live urban environment at Punggol Digital District. The testbed will deploy AI-driven delivery, cleaning, and security patrol robots alongside human workers, marking a major real-world agentic AI deployment in Southeast Asia. (Published: 2026-05-21)

🔐 Cybersecurity

HDFC AMC Cybersecurity Incident Under Continued Investigation

HDFC Asset Management Company's cybersecurity breach, first disclosed on May 18 after an anonymous tip-off about possible unauthorized IT system access on May 16, remained under active forensic investigation as of May 21. The company engaged a specialist firm and activated containment protocols; it stated no material impact on investment management operations or evidence of customer data loss so far. Shares of HDFC AMC fell 2.3% to ₹2,645.9 following the disclosure, with market experts urging caution until the full investigation concludes. (Published: 2026-05-21)

Singapore Updates Agentic AI Governance Framework with Security Guidelines

As part of ATxSummit 2026, Singapore updated its Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI with new case studies from over 50 organisations including AWS, DBS, Google, and Salesforce. The update addresses security vulnerabilities in agentic AI systems — including prompt injection and multi-agent trust chains — and integrates post-quantum cryptography readiness guidance. The Cyber Security Agency of Singapore also published companion security guidance for organisations deploying autonomous AI agents. (Published: 2026-05-21)

🚀 Deeptech Startups & Investments

Piper Serica Launches ₹800 Crore Bharat Tech Fund for Deeptech Startups

Mumbai-based asset manager Piper Serica launched its Bharat Tech Fund, a Category II AIF targeting ₹600 crore with a ₹200 crore greenshoe option, totalling ₹800 crore (~$96M). The fund will write ₹25–50 crore cheques into Series A and B deeptech startups spanning semiconductors, AI, spacetech, defence tech, biosciences, and fintech infrastructure. Piper Serica will source deals from IIT Madras, IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay, IISc Bangalore, iDEX, IN-SPACe, and DRDO, using its in-house AI screening tool Yoda.ai. (Published: 2026-05-21)

Scapia Raises $63 Million Series C Led by General Catalyst, Valuation Doubles to $500M+

Bengaluru-based travel-fintech Scapia closed a $63 million Series C round led by General Catalyst, with existing investors Peak XV Partners and Z47 participating. The all-equity round more than doubles Scapia's valuation to over $500 million from ~$200 million in April 2025. Total funding now stands at $135 million. Scapia, which offers an AI-first travel credit card in partnership with Federal Bank and BOBCARD, reported 5–6x growth in flight bookings and 8x growth in hotel stays over the past year. India Startup Funding Hits 658% Weekly Surge (Week of May 15–21)

The week ending May 21, 2026 saw Indian startup investments reach $354 million — a 658.1% spike over the previous week and 178.98% above the week before that — marking one of the sharpest single-week surges in recent memory. Key contributors included Scapia ($63M), UpGrad ($38M), and multiple seed rounds in AI, healthtech, and climate sectors. Southeast Asian deeptech startups were simultaneously reported to be looking to Japan for capital and market access, as regional funding remains subdued with total deal volume at 461 deals in 2025, down 27% year-on-year. 📜 Tech Policy & Regulation

India's 2026 IT Rules: AI Content Labeling Mandate Takes Shape

India's updated IT Amendment Rules, which took effect February 20, 2026, continue to be scrutinised and debated by stakeholders. The rules mandate that all AI-generated content be declared and labeled, and require social media platforms to remove harmful content within 3 hours. On May 21, industry bodies flagged clarity concerns about the scope of "AI-generated content" and enforcement timelines, with MeitY officials assuring flexibility in implementation during a public consultation session. 💡 Key Takeaways

1. Singapore is moving fastest on agentic AI governance. ATxSummit 2026's closing day brought landmark partnerships (NVIDIA lab, OpenAI MOU, Google AI partnership) and a live agentic AI testbed — reinforcing Singapore's role as the region's AI governance and innovation hub.

2. India's deeptech funding is accelerating sharply. The 658% weekly funding surge and Piper Serica's ₹800 Cr deeptech-focused fund signal growing institutional conviction in India's semiconductor, spacetech, and AI sectors.

3. Financial sector cybersecurity in India remains under pressure. The HDFC AMC breach investigation ongoing on May 21 underscores that Indian financial institutions are prime targets, with the WEF ranking cybersecurity as India's #1 national risk for 2026.

4. Travel-fintech is India's new unicorn-maker. Scapia's $500M+ valuation and $63M raise show that AI-first fintech startups targeting India's travel boom are attracting top-tier global capital.

5. Southeast Asia's deeptech gap widens. While India surges, SEA deeptech startups are turning to Japan for capital and growth — a sign that the regional funding environment remains challenging despite strong underlying innovation.
