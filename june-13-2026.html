Cybersecurity & Deep Tech Security

Pramatra Space builds quantum-resilient communication with an in-house photonics chip

Bengaluru-based Pramatra Space is tackling the looming "Q-Day" risk — the point at which quantum computers could break today's encryption. The startup has developed an in-house photonics chip that generates encryption keys using quantum entanglement, aiming to make enterprises quantum-resilient. Founded in 2023 by Richa Hukumchand (ex-DRDO scientist and former Pixxel CIO) and Vinay Hukumchand, the company has booked an in-orbit demonstration mission for its payload in 2027. (Published: 2026-06-12)

Vehant Technologies launches "MilliView," an indigenous mmWave body scanner

Vehant Technologies, with IIT Delhi, launched MilliView, a made-in-India millimetre-wave body scanner for airports, security agencies and government bodies. Using non-ionising mmWave technology and AI-based threat recognition, it detects metallic and non-metallic concealed threats — including explosives, detonators and gel-based substances — in under five seconds, using a gender-neutral avatar to preserve passenger privacy. Vehant plans to pursue European certification for international expansion. (Published: 2026-06-12)
