Cybersecurity

Anthropic Expands Project Glasswing to India and 14 Other Countries

Anthropic expanded its cyber-defence initiative Project Glasswing to 150 organisations across more than 15 countries, with India among the newly added nations. The expansion grants access to Anthropic's Mythos AI model — designed to discover software vulnerabilities — to partners in power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware sectors. Since Glasswing launched in April, the initial 50-strong membership has used Mythos to uncover over 10,000 high-or-critical-severity vulnerabilities. The newly expanded cohort also includes vendors whose codebases underpin government and enterprise systems globally. (Published: 2026-06-03)

India's BFSI Sector Exposed to AI-Driven Cyber Attacks

India's banking, financial services, and insurance sector faces an acute cybersecurity risk as shared vendor ecosystems and slow cyber response cycles create systemic vulnerabilities. India's banking sector absorbed 2.72 billion attacks last year, with DDoS attempts rising 2.7x and vulnerability exploits rising 2.5x year-on-year. The sector now experiences cyberattacks at 1.6 times the global average. CERT-In has called for a shift toward continuous exposure management, rapid remediation, and real-time validation of security controls in response to the AI-accelerated threat landscape. (Published: 2026-06-03)

India's AI Chip Ecosystem Faces Strategic Pressure as Taiwan and South Korea Race Ahead

India slipped to seventh in global equity market capitalisation rankings as AI-led semiconductor rallies in Taiwan ($4.95 trillion) and South Korea ($5 trillion) pushed both markets ahead of India's $4.8 trillion cap. Despite strong momentum in semiconductor policy and the India Semiconductor Mission, experts warn India remains primarily an AI consumer rather than an AI infrastructure producer. Tech Policy & Regulation

India Government Plans "AI-Against-AI" Strategy to Protect Digital Public Infrastructure

India's government is preparing to deploy artificial intelligence offensively to defend its digital public infrastructure — a "fight fire with fire" approach to cyber threats. A joint task force comprising the Ministry of Finance, the Reserve Bank of India, and MeitY is coordinating the initiative. The strategy acknowledges that advanced AI models have lowered the barrier for cybercriminals to exploit critical systems, and that India's existing periodic-audit approach is no longer sufficient. The announcement follows CERT-In's May 25 Blueprint on AI-assisted vulnerability exploitation. (Published: 2026-06-03)

Key Takeaways

India is moving into cyberdefence at pace. Three separate developments on June 3 — Anthropic Glasswing expansion, CERT-In's continuous operations push, and the government's "AI-against-AI" strategy — signal that India is converging on a coherent AI-powered cyber posture for both public and private infrastructure.

Deeptech funding remains active despite macro caution. SAP Labs' 16-startup cohort, ProLearn's ₹30 crore pre-seed, and the broader June 3 roundup show investors and corporates continuing to back foundational AI and deeptech bets in India even as late-stage deal volumes have pulled back.

Southeast Asia is no longer experimenting. Singapore's ATxEnterprise 2026 crystallised a regional shift: the policy and enterprise frameworks are now in place, and the next phase is execution at scale across ASEAN.

The semiconductor gap is a strategic risk. India's drop to 7th in global market cap amid AI-led chip rallies in Taiwan and South Korea is a concrete reminder that AI value creation is shifting toward hardware. India's policy response — the India Semiconductor Mission — is in motion, but execution speed will determine whether India remains an AI consumer or becomes an AI infrastructure producer.

AI observability is the next infrastructure layer. Coralogix's $200M raise on June 3 underscores that as AI agents proliferate across India and Southeast Asia, the monitoring and security layer for those agents is becoming mission-critical infrastructure.
