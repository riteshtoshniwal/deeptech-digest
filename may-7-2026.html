🚀 INDIA DEEPTECH & SPACE

GalaxEye Launches Mission Drishti — World's First OptoSAR Satellite

Indian space startup GalaxEye, incubated at IIT Madras, launched Mission Drishti aboard SpaceX's Falcon 9 rocket from Vandenberg Space Force Base, California on May 3, 2026. The 190 kg satellite is the world's first to integrate Optical and SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) imaging on a single platform — capable of all-weather, round-the-clock Earth observation at 1.2–3.6m resolution from a 500 km orbit. Drishti is designed for agriculture monitoring, disaster management, maritime tracking, and defence surveillance. GalaxEye envisions this as the first node in a 10-satellite "Drishti Constellation" to be completed by 2029.

🛡️ CYBERSECURITY — INDIA & REGION

Silver Fox APT Campaign Targets Indian Organizations with New Malware

A China-linked advanced persistent threat (APT) group dubbed Silver Fox launched a sophisticated phishing campaign against organisations in India and Russia, using tax-themed emails to deliver the newly identified ABCDoor backdoor alongside known ValleyRAT malware. Researchers at Kaspersky observed over 1,600 malicious messages, using spoofed government notices and malicious archives to gain persistent remote access. The campaign underscores growing state-sponsored cyber operations targeting Indian enterprises ahead of tax filing seasons.

India Ranks as Cyberattack Hotspot: 2,011 Weekly Attacks on Average

According to Check Point Software Technologies' State of Cyber Security in India 2025 report, Indian organisations faced an average of 2,011 cyberattacks per week — significantly above the global average. The World Economic Forum's Global Risk Report 2026 now ranks cybersecurity as India's number one national risk, ahead of climate disasters and armed conflict. Key threat vectors include cloud misconfigurations, infostealer malware, ransomware, and AI-enabled fraud.

Operation TrueChaos: Zero-Day Attack on Southeast Asian Government Networks

Check Point Research documented Operation TrueChaos — a zero-day exploitation campaign attributed to a Chinese-linked threat actor that targeted Southeast Asian government networks. Compromising a single server was sufficient to propagate malware across dozens of connected government agencies simultaneously, exposing critical weaknesses in shared government IT infrastructure across the region.

📜 POLICY & REGULATION

India's 2026 IT Rules: AI Content Labeling Now Mandatory

India's 2026 IT Amendment Rules mandate that all AI-generated content hosted or transmitted on digital platforms must be labeled. Social media platforms must remove non-consensual intimate imagery (including AI-generated deepfakes) within 2 hours and other harmful AI-generated content within 3 hours. The rules complement India's broader AI Governance Guidelines, which take a risk-based, light-touch approach, and align with the newly established AI Safety Institute (AISI) and AI Governance Group (AIGG). Foreign platforms operating in India now face legal risk under the new compliance framework.
