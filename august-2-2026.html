Cybersecurity

Nagpur Consumer Commission Holds ICICI Bank Liable in "Digital Arrest" Fraud Case

The Nagpur District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission ordered ICICI Bank to refund Rs 5,18,437 plus 9% interest to a victim of a FedEx/fake-police "digital arrest" scam, ruling that OTP-authorised transactions do not absolve the bank of its RBI-mandated duty to monitor suspicious accounts. The beneficiary account had processed roughly Rs 2.84 crore in two days without triggering scrutiny — a significant consumer-liability precedent for Indian banks on cyber fraud. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- The420.in

Prime Accused Arrested in Telangana's Rs 52 Crore Investment Fraud

Telangana Police arrested Bethampudi Bharat Kumar from Kottayam, Kerala, in an alleged Rs 52 crore investment fraud run through fake partnership agreements and forged documents; 35 vehicles and real estate purchased with the proceeds were seized. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- The420.in

Hackers Poison Adform Ad-Tech Script to Swap Crypto Wallet Addresses

The Hacker News reported that attackers compromised a JavaScript file from ad-tech provider Adform, injecting code that swapped cryptocurrency wallet addresses across customer websites — a supply-chain attack with implications for any enterprise (including in India/SEA) loading third-party ad scripts. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- The Hacker News

Adobe Patches CVSS 10.0 Flaw in Campaign Classic

Adobe issued a patch for CVE-2026-48449, a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) vulnerability in Adobe Campaign Classic that allows code execution without user interaction. Enterprises running the marketing platform are urged to update immediately. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- The Hacker News

Zepto to Re-Attempt IPO by May 2027

After shelving its 2026 listing amid valuation disagreements, CEO Aadit Palicha told employees Zepto now aims to list within two to three quarters (by May 2027). The quick-commerce major is meanwhile pursuing a roughly Rs 1,000 Cr pre-IPO round at a moderated ~$4.5 Bn valuation to raise domestic shareholding. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- Inc42

Shadowfax Raises FY27 Revenue Guidance After Stellar Q1

On its Q1 FY27 earnings call, listed logistics firm Shadowfax raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to 38–40% (from 28–30%) after Q1 PAT jumped 8X YoY to Rs 65.4 Cr and revenue rose 65% to Rs 1,358 Cr. Its Prime Large heavy-shipments arm grew 170% YoY to ~Rs 75 Cr ARR. (Published: 2026-08-01)
- Inc42

Key Takeaways

  1. Weekly funding cooled sharply but AI still leads. Indian startup funding fell ~63% week-on-week to $142 Mn, yet the two largest deals (Freehand, Smallest.ai) were both AI companies — the AI premium in Indian venture funding persists even in slow weeks.
  2. A consumer-liability precedent on cyber fraud. The Nagpur ruling against ICICI Bank signals that Indian banks cannot hide behind OTP authorisation in "digital arrest" scam cases — expect increased pressure on banks to strengthen transaction monitoring.
  3. Supply-chain attacks remain the sharpest enterprise threat. The Adform script poisoning and the CVSS 10.0 Adobe Campaign Classic flaw are both reminders that third-party code and marketing infrastructure are prime attack surfaces.
  4. Listed new-age tech is maturing. Shadowfax's raised guidance and the broad weekly gains across new-age tech stocks point to improving fundamentals among India's listed startups, with IPO pipelines (Zepto by May 2027) rebuilding.
  5. Quiet Saturday for SEA. No Southeast Asia-specific funding, policy, or breach news could be confirmed for August 1 — regional coverage should normalise with the new week.
