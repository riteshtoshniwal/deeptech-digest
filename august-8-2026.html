AI and Machine Learning

OpenAI Deepens India Push with Voice AI, Indic Languages and Lower Pricing

OpenAI strengthened its India strategy with an emphasis on Indic languages and voice AI, and slashed prices of its GPT-5.6 models to attract developers and companies seeking affordable AI solutions. The move intensifies competition for India's fast-growing developer and enterprise AI market. (Published: 2026-08-07)

Acrab's US$130 Mn Raise Signals Southeast Asia's Deeper Push into AI Hardware

Singapore-based Acrab, which is building an agentic AI compute platform combining custom silicon, edge AI models and software orchestration, closed a $130 Mn Series B led by Vertex Ventures SEA & India and Vertex Growth, taking cumulative funding past $480 Mn. Analysis published on 7 August argued the raise marks Southeast Asia's shift from AI applications toward core AI hardware and infrastructure. (Published: 2026-08-07)

Deep Tech Startups and Investments

Solinas Integrity Raises $5.5 Mn Series A1 for Underground Infrastructure Robotics

Chennai-based, IIT Madras-incubated Solinas Integrity raised $5.5 Mn (~₹46 crore) in a Series A1 round led by Hero Enterprise Partner Ventures and Mela Ventures, with Luthra Group joining and follow-on from SVL-SME Fund, Rainmatter and 8X Ventures. The startup builds AI-powered robots for inspecting and cleaning underground water and wastewater networks, deployed across 35+ cities in India, and plans expansion into the Middle East and Southeast Asia. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- StartupTalky

Mitti Labs Bags $9.5 Mn Series A from Aramco Ventures for Climate-Tech Rice Farming

Climate tech startup Mitti Labs raised $9.5 Mn (~₹90 crore) in a Series A led by Aramco Ventures, with Godrej Industries Group, Cisco Foundation, Volta Circle and Lightspeed India participating. The startup helps rice farmers adopt alternate wetting and drying irrigation — cutting water use ~40% and methane emissions by more than half — and will expand across India plus greenfield projects in the Philippines and Indonesia. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- StartupTalky

BlissClub Closes ₹160 Crore Series B Led by Singularity AMC

Bengaluru-based D2C athleisure brand BlissClub raised ₹160 crore (~$16.8 Mn) in a Series B led by Singularity AMC, with founder Minu Margeret, Vidit Aatrey, Elevation Capital and Eight Roads Ventures participating. The capital will fund new categories, offline retail expansion and product development after FY25 revenue grew 51% to ₹131.5 crore. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- StartupTalky

Weekly VC Inflow into Indian Startups Jumps to $323 Mn, Led by River Mobility

YourStory's weekly funding roundup published on 7 August reported that Indian startups raised $323 Mn across 25 deals during 1–7 August, a sharp rise after VC funding had dropped below $100 Mn in late July. EV startup River Mobility's $120 Mn raise provided the biggest lift. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- YourStory

Ola Electric Q1 FY27: Loss Narrows to ₹336 Crore as Market Share Recovers

Ola Electric narrowed its net loss 22% YoY to ₹336 crore in Q1 FY27, though operating revenue fell 45% YoY to ₹455 crore (up 72% sequentially). The company's e2W market share improved to 8.4% from 5.1% in Q4 FY26, and it is targeting a ₹300 crore per quarter steady-state cost base on the path to operating breakeven. (Published: 2026-08-07)

Cybersecurity

An intelligence briefing published on 7 August highlighted that attackers are increasingly compromising trusted systems and external service providers — including ransomware-as-a-service ecosystems, government registries and third-party platforms supporting retail and healthcare — to maximise disruption and data exposure. The same briefing noted Ashoka University and ESET awarded the 2026 ESET Women in Cybersecurity Scholarship in India to Urvashi Balasubramaniam, part of efforts to grow India's cybersecurity talent pipeline. Regional Tech Policy and Regulations

RBI Bans Banks from Remotely Locking Borrowers' Phones to Recover Loans

The Reserve Bank of India has forbidden banks from remotely locking borrowers' mobile phones, tablets or laptops to recover loans, except under certain device-financing arrangements. Where permitted, restrictions may only take effect after 30 days of default and must be phased in over 60 days under the new standards — a significant consumer-protection move for India's digital lending ecosystem. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- StartupTalky

US Imposes 15% Polysilicon Tariff, Pressuring Asia's Chip and Solar Supply Chains

The US announced a 15% tariff and minimum import prices on polysilicon, targeting China's dominance of solar and semiconductor supply chains. The move has direct implications for India and Southeast Asia's electronics and solar manufacturing ambitions as global buyers reassess sourcing, potentially accelerating supply-chain diversification into the region. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- StartupTalky

Notable Tech Industry Events

Vietnam's Science Ministry and Singapore's NUS Enterprise Sign AI and Semiconductor Pact

Vietnam's Ministry of Science and Technology and NUS Enterprise (National University of Singapore) agreed to collaborate on artificial intelligence, semiconductors, technology transfer, startup development and tech workforce training. Cooperation commences in August 2026, with initial results expected by year-end — a notable cross-border deeptech alliance within ASEAN. (Published: 2026-08-07)
- TNGlobal / TechNode Global

Key Takeaways

  1. India's deeptech capital cycle is broadening. The day's funding — robotics for water infrastructure (Solinas) and methane-cutting agritech (Mitti Labs, backed by Aramco Ventures) — shows hard-tech and climate deeptech pulling in strategic and global corporate capital, not just VC. Weekly inflows rebounding to $323 Mn confirms momentum after a weak late-July.

  2. Southeast Asia is moving down the AI stack. Acrab's $130 Mn Series B and the Vietnam–NUS semiconductor pact both point to the region investing in AI hardware, silicon and compute infrastructure rather than only applications — a structural shift for ASEAN's tech economy.

  3. Global AI players are pricing for India. OpenAI's Indic-language, voice-first, lower-priced push underscores India's role as the key volume market for AI adoption, and will pressure domestic model builders on price and local-language capability.

  4. Regulators are tightening consumer tech protections. RBI's ban on device-locking for loan recovery is a reminder that India's digital lending and device-financing models face rising conduct scrutiny, while US polysilicon tariffs add new supply-chain policy risk for Asia's chip and solar sectors.

  5. Supply-chain and third-party cyber risk dominates. The day's threat intelligence emphasised attacks routed through trusted vendors and service providers — a warning for India and SEA enterprises to prioritise third-party risk management ahead of DPDP enforcement.
